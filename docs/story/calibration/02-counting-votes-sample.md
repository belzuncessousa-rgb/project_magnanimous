## The first request

Livia takes a second sheet from her folder and slides it across the coffee table towards you. A list of departments occupies most of the page, with the requested number of appointments beside each one.

> **Livia:** "There's one appointment we should discuss while we're all here. The Port Authority of Katanaí."

> **Mertens:** "Odel has been asking me about that since the election."

> **Livia:** "He would like to nominate the director and fill about forty positions in the authority. Most of them are supervisory posts."

> **Mertens:** "He originally asked for sixty."

Livia gives him a stern look.

> **Livia:** "Fifty-two."

> **Mertens:** "Did he? Well, we got him down to forty."

He smiles at her, but she is already looking back at the list.

The port was at the centre of the bribery scandal that brought down the previous government. You recognise several of the departments on the page.

## Decision

1. **"Let the OPP nominate all forty officials and the director."**

   Livia draws the sheet back towards her and writes a note at the top.

   > **Livia:** "I'll ask for the names today."

   Mertens looks pleased.

   > **Mertens:** "I'll speak to Odel about the budget this afternoon."

2. **"Keep the director independent. Consider the other nominees on merit."**

   Mertens picks up the sheet and studies the list, his brow slightly furrowed.

   > **Mertens:** "He'll want to know which posts he's actually getting."

   > **Livia:** "Then he can send us the candidates and we'll tell him."

   He reads the list once more.

   > **Mertens:** "Fine. Give me a day with him."

3. **"No agreement until we've negotiated the budget."**

   > **Mertens:** "I can take that back to him. He may want a little more by then."

   Livia makes a small note.

   > **Livia:** "I'll keep the appointments on hold."

4. **"No political appointments at the port."**

   Mertens's smile fades. He looks down at the list for a few seconds.

   > **Mertens:** "All right. We'll have to find him something else."

   > **Livia:** "I'll have the relevant positions filled through the usual process."

   He folds his copy of the list and puts it into his jacket pocket. When he speaks again, his tone is more serious.

   > **Mertens:** "If there are other restrictions on what I can negotiate, Lara, I'd like to know about them before I go back to Congress."

   Optional immediate response (one):

   - **"I'll consider your proposals. Just not this one."**

     He nods, looking a little less concerned.

     > **Mertens:** "Fair enough."

   - **"I don't intend to trade public offices for votes."**

     > **Mertens:** "Then we'd better find something the parties want that you're willing to give them."

     Livia looks up from her notes.

     > **Livia:** "I'll make sure that is clear in the next round of negotiations."

   - **"We'll discuss that another time."**

     > **Mertens:** "Of course."
