## The residence

By the time you reach the presidential residence, it is well after dark. The house stands in a garden behind the palace, a short drive from the offices where you have spent the afternoon. A member of staff shows you the bedroom and tells you where to find the telephone numbers for the night shift.

When you return to the sitting room, Amanda is kneeling beside the coffee table, taking food out of a paper bag.

You have known Amanda Troes since law school. She worked with you for much of your career and now serves as your personal secretary. Today, she has spent most of her time arranging meetings and dealing with the people who want to see you.

> **Amanda:** "Have you eaten?"

She looks up at you.

> **Amanda:** "I bought some sandwiches on the way here. I wasn't sure what they'd have in the kitchen."

Player chooses:

- **"After today, I could eat both of them."**

  Amanda laughs and hands you the bag.

  > **Amanda:** "Take the one on top. I don't like the cheese in that one."

- **"You know we have a kitchen staff now, right?"**

  > **Amanda:** "I know. I haven't worked out who to ask for food yet. I thought I'd give the poor people a day to settle in."

- **"I'm not hungry, but sit with me for a while."**

  > **Amanda:** "Of course."

  She puts the bag aside and takes a seat.

## An old memory

Amanda takes off her shoes and stretches her legs out beneath the coffee table. On the television, with the volume turned down, a news presenter is standing in front of the palace.

> **Amanda:** "Do you remember the suit you wore when we went to look for our first internships?"

She smiles as she looks at the television.

> **Amanda:** "You had to lend me your jacket for my interview. It didn't fit me at all."

Player chooses:

- **"You got the job, though."**

  > **Amanda:** "Yes! They even asked me to start on Monday. I had to call you and ask if I could keep the jacket for another week."

- **"And I had to wait in the corridor in my shirt."**

  > **Amanda:** "I told you to go home! You insisted on waiting for me."

  She laughs.

- **"That was a long time ago."**

  > **Amanda:** "It was."

  She watches the television for a moment.

## Before she goes

Amanda checks the time on her phone.

> **Amanda:** "I should probably go. I told my mother I'd call her tonight, and I'm already late."

She reaches for her shoes.
