# First-term scene inventory

Each numbered row lists the encounters assigned to a turn. Links identify the finished player-facing prose by file or heading. Conditional lines in the manuscripts are author-facing dispatch instructions and must not appear in dialogue or narration. The order within a turn is flexible where prerequisites permit it.

## Calendar and prerequisites

- **Opening.** The inaugural address opens the term. Blue Room and Counting Votes introduce the cabinet and legislature. The Diary follows Counting Votes, and First Night is available after the Diary. Skipping a personal scene does not skip the following turn. A port negotiation can be deferred without creating an appointment or a completed agreement.
- **Decisions that close a turn.** The first investment round asks Lara to choose from the prepared appraisals. Later budgeting, project, and legislative decisions can resolve through submission, deferral, withdrawal, or infeasibility. Receiving a proposal creates no obligation to accept it. A missing offer or an ineligible project does not leave the calendar waiting for an impossible scene.
- **Follow-ups.** A conditional row is an eligibility rule, not an additional turn. Schedule its scene at the next available point after the trigger. When a numbered row also names a follow-up, treat that row as the earliest intended slot. One award, vacancy, inquiry result, or signed text receives one corresponding encounter unless a genuinely new result creates another. Optional encounters can expire and never satisfy a different scene's prerequisite.
- **Reusable project scenes.** A project heading may appear in more than one turn because a project can have distinct awards or delivery phases. Bind each dispatch to the project identity and phase. Play a signature encounter once per newly authorized award. Reuse the delivery encounter only for a newly usable, separately recorded scope; a report about an already operating section is not a new opening.
- **Departing officeholders.** Resolve any resignation or appointment at its effective time. Once Livia leaves as Chief of Staff after an OPP ballot, she appears only in her party role; complete any still-eligible Chief of Staff handover before scenes requiring that office. Role-based staff dialogue in the hearing, military crisis, and campaign transition uses the current Chief of Staff or acting deputy.
- **Campaign.** Final project and programme results are independent of the nomination. Resolve Lara's decision to stand, any viable party process and commission registration before opening candidate-only campaign scenes. A candidacy remains on the ballot until the commission confirms a valid withdrawal; ending campaign appearances alone does not remove her name or preselect the result. Defer the public-record encounter while a viable nomination remains pending; after registration it prepares campaign use, and after Lara declines, has an accepted ballot withdrawal, fails to register or exhausts viable candidacies it prepares the handover. The coalition programme requires a confirmed nominee and willing partner. An effective early ending supersedes the regular handover.
