# Governing after midterm

## The signature page

*After site and procurement discussions. Use the actual contract authority and funding available for the identified project and phase. Play once per distinct project award, including a separate second-round project. Bind the dispatch to the project ID and round so an existing project or work order cannot reopen the award scene. Procurement intent and announced financing do not qualify a bid. If continuing under an existing agreement, use an authorized work order and omit new-award choices.*

The planning director has clipped the signature page to the front. A representative from the selected site waits at the other end of the table. She keeps looking at a copy with several dates underlined.

> **Lara:** “Which of those dates are you concerned about?”

> **Representative:** “The first one. It's the date we would have to tell people to make other arrangements. We'd like a name to give them if the work starts late.”

Marne opens the agreed scope. On the railway, he points out the first section the award covers. On sanitation, he gives the addresses whose connections are included. On the mine, he puts the agreed access and monitoring conditions next to the work order. For the hub, water programme, and fluvial network, show the funded facility, supply phase, or landing and service commitments already negotiated.

> **Marne:** “The next phase is on a separate page. The local office should get this one first, with the dates the contractor has agreed to.”

### Approve the award and independent checks

*Show with a viable offer and an authorized review that can be added to the terms.*

> **Lara:** “Approve these terms. Give the review team access to the work before the next payment.”

Marne adds the agreed review date to the copy for the site. “I'll ask the contractor to send the local office the name of the person the inspectors should call.”

Record the award once all required signatures are present. A review can establish findings or prevent an avoidable cost. A promised publication is fulfilled only when the releasable documents are published. Set the legacy `luz_works_resumed` state only on actual resumption of those works, regardless of the route that authorized it.

### Approve the faster eligible offer

*Show with an actual shorter schedule and the same required statutory safeguards. Explain which additional checks can be performed later.*

> **Lara:** “Use the earlier start date. Give me the inspection dates with it.”

Marne hands the representative the site schedule. She copies the contact number onto her own sheet. “I'll wait until your office confirms the signature before I give this to the residents.”

### Direct an eligible preferred award

*Show the actual alternative and whether it is a lawful preference or a departure from the required award process. Only offer the latter where an official with effective authority can carry out Lara's instruction.*

> **Lara:** “Send the award to the bidder we've discussed. Put the terms of the work in the schedule.”

Marne asks the clerk to bring the revised recommendation. If the instruction departs from required procedure, the clerk asks who is authorizing the change before accepting it for filing. The record may later enter an inquiry. Use the actual award terms to determine delivery and any benefit.

### Issue the funded work order under existing terms

*Show only where the contract already covers the work and the legal review permits it.*

> **Lara:** “Release the work that's covered here. Leave the remaining streets on the review list.”

For a project other than sanitation, name the specific remaining works. Marne gives the clerk the existing contract reference. “They'll need both this number and the order. The office at the site should have the same papers.”

### Hold the signature

> **Lara:** “Hold it until the authority and funding are in place. Tell the site what can still go ahead.”

The representative draws a line through the first proposed date. “I'll leave the notice up for the work already running. When should I call about this one?”

If nothing is funded, send a status notice without an opening date. Advance only through a later authorized award and contract commencement.

Play when a distinct section or service has begun operating, not when the project merely passes another administrative milestone. Bind the report to the project ID and delivery phase. Do not replay an already reported opening; later progress, remaining work and corrections use the appropriate report or incident follow-up.*

## Where the referrals go

*After the budget authorizes a new anti-corruption unit or a separate, lawful decision creates capacity for the work. A proposal or a funding line alone does not give the unit investigative powers. If the initiative fails to obtain funding, use the existing-office exchange only.*

Salgado brings the form the departments already use when they send her office a complaint. She puts the draft form for the new unit beside it.

> **Salgado:** “The ministries are asking where to send the next file. I can answer that after we agree who can decide whether it goes forward.”

> **Lara:** “What would stay with your office?”

> **Salgado:** “The work the law assigns to us. I want the new office to record why it takes a case and where it sends it. The clerk needs to be able to give the same answer whether the complaint names a minister or somebody in a district office.”

- **“Give the funded unit its independent referral authority.”** Show only the authority the government can lawfully establish. Salgado takes the draft form. “I'll send the departments the new address as soon as the office can receive a file.” Its eventual reach depends on staffing and effective safeguards.
- **“Keep referral decisions with the current authority.”** Salgado marks the existing form. “They can send the material to us. If the additional staff are available, they can help examine it under the present arrangements.” The current offices retain their responsibilities.
- **“Bring significant referrals through the ministry first.”** Show only if that arrangement can actually be imposed, and explain what it changes. Salgado sets both forms down. “If you're asking the ministry to decide which cases can go forward, I want that instruction in writing.” An independent body can still act on evidence it already holds. Such a decision can affect Salgado's willingness to remain in office under her established departure conditions.

*If there is no funding for the unit:* Salgado puts the existing form on top. “We'll have to keep receiving these through the current offices. I'll ask them to tell us which files they can deal with this month.” Lara can request that list or choose the case review the existing office already has authority to perform.

*Once the arrangement is operating,* an official at the registry gives a complainant a receipt. The official tells her which office can acknowledge the referral, and where she should ask about its status. This creates capacity to handle cases. It does not decide an allegation.

## What the accountant kept

*Only with evidence of a procurement irregularity or credible allegation. Renata distinguishes what the material proves from what remains to investigate.*

Renata gives you three pages. A signature appears on two of them.

> **Renata:** “The invoice came in twice. One copy has a different supplier name. The payments went through on different days.”

Use this line only when the copies and payment entries are available. If the allegation is supported by an incomplete record, use: “These are the two references the accountant gave us. We have a copy of one invoice. The payment office hasn't confirmed the second entry.” In either version, the recurring reference does not yet establish what was billed or whether anyone acted unlawfully.

> **Lara:** “Who brought you the second copy?”

> **Renata:** “A project accountant. She asked whether she'd still have a job on Monday. I told her I'd take the question to you.”

- **“Seek protection for her and refer the records.”** Renata gives the exhibits to the clerk. “Please get a receipt in her name. I'll ask the protection office whether it can give her a decision before Monday.” Protection and referral take effect separately. A referral establishes neither guilt nor recovery.
- **“Audit it within the ministry first.”** Renata takes out a receipt for the copies she leaves with you. “Ask the auditors to name the records they've seen. If they get only the first copy, somebody should be able to tell us that.” The audit may confirm the discrepancy, explain it, or stall. An independent auditor can still refer the matter.
- **“Keep the material inside the government.”** Show only where Lara can restrict circulation. Renata leaves her hand on the receipt. “When the accountant calls, I can tell her we accepted the papers. What am I supposed to tell her about the review?” Lara can authorize an internal review, tell Renata to report the current status, or leave the accountant without a further answer from the executive. A body already holding independent evidence retains it.

*If she still faces action at work:* “I've been asked to come in without my supervisor. They said I could bring someone. Can you tell me which office has my question?”

- **“Tell her the status ourselves.”** Lara or the clerk explains what was accepted and what decision is still pending. Do not promise job protection that the government lacks authority to provide.

The accountant asks whether she should retain the receipt. The clerk tells her which reference to give if she needs to call again. If she has withdrawn from contact or another authority is dealing with her, carry forward only what the government actually learns.

## Whose roads stay open

*Regional development meeting in Mognagna. Use confirmed drought and water-service information. A local intermediary attends only where he controls a relevant distribution network.*

A municipal engineer arrives with the delivery list written on the back of an old route sheet. She reads two village names aloud. The local organizer beside her corrects the pronunciation of one.

> **Engineer:** “Neither delivery got through yesterday.”

> **Organizer:** “The driver stopped at the estate. That's where he collects the second load.”

> **Lara:** “Who decides where he goes after that?”

The engineer turns the sheet around so you can see the empty spaces beside both villages.

- **“Put the municipality in charge and publish the daily deliveries.”** Provide staff and capacity where available. Early coverage may be uneven where the old network withdraws help.
- **“Use the existing routes under a temporary agreement.”** The organizer turns the route sheet around. “I can get a truck to both villages today. I need to know who settles a dispute about the list.” Lara chooses the terms for the temporary arrangement:
  - **“The municipality approves changes and publishes the daily list.”** The organizer agrees to send the routes to the engineer before dispatch. The municipality can answer questions about where the next load is going, but it needs staff to keep up with the reports. If it cannot provide them, the delivery record remains incomplete.
  - **“Let the organizer set the route through the end of the dry season.”** He folds the route sheet along the old crease. “Then I can use the drivers we already have. I'll tell the villages when the next load is coming.” Coverage can improve quickly, while decisions and local influence remain with his network.
  - **“Decline the temporary agreement.”** The organizer puts the sheet back in his folder. “Then your office needs its own trucks before Friday.” The municipality can proceed only at the capacity it has confirmed; any uncovered village remains on the relief list.
- **“Prioritize permanent water work.”** If funded, the engineer confirms when surveying can start. Current shortages still need an answer. Lara can authorize temporary relief if there is money left.

## A place to go, a reason to stay

*Conditional regional planning scene after a confirmed Mognagna water or livelihood review. Offer relocation only when a receiving municipality has agreed to assess capacity and the responsible ministry has costed voluntary assistance. No household is moved without its informed agreement. Relocation is an alternative to long-term water infrastructure, not a substitute for emergency food or water relief.*

The meeting is in a school hall. A clerk has pinned a map to the wall, and someone has drawn a line from the dry interior to three towns in other regions. The line ends at the edge of each town; there are no marks for houses or arrival dates.

> **Clerk:** “These are places that asked for an estimate. None of them has offered a house.”

> **Resident:** “My sister went to Moreya last year. She found work. Her boy had to change schools twice before they found a room they could afford.”

The municipal engineer sets down a second map, this one showing water points that can be repaired. The cost estimate covers only the first stage. An organizer who has been quiet until now asks whether people who stay will still be on the delivery list.

> **Lara:** “Yes. A family choosing to stay does not lose its place in the relief plan.”

The ministry's estimate separates travel and settling support from housing, school places, and local services in each receiving town. Those costs would fall on different budgets. The receiving municipalities have agreed to review applications, not to accept an unlimited number of arrivals. The clerk has brought two forms: one to request more information, and one to register interest without committing to move.

- **“Fund a voluntary relocation offer and ask each receiving town for a capacity limit.”** Publish eligibility, travel support, school and service arrangements, and a way to withdraw before departure. The ministry returns with the number of households that choose to apply and the receiving towns' actual capacity. The sending communities lose residents only if they decide to go; receiving areas face temporary pressure on stability and public services. Do not count an application as a completed move.
- **“Keep people in place and bring me a costed water network.”** The engineer marks the first repairs and the households they can reach. Lara may fund the work if authorized. The project addresses the underlying access problem over time; emergency delivery and food support continue separately while it is built.
- **“Keep the present relief and delivery arrangements.”** The clerk leaves both maps on the table. Current assistance remains under its actual terms. No relocation offer or new infrastructure work begins, and the next review uses verified access, prices, and household requests.

If Lara approves relocation planning, follow only when applications, receiving-town capacity, and funding are confirmed. At the later report, state how many households moved, remained, or withdrew; do not treat regional education or infrastructure changes as immediate or universal. If the plan cannot be funded or no receiving town agrees, return that finding and continue the existing relief choices.

## The title on the table

*Conditional land-policy scene after a documented Mognagna land claim or a funded review of the regional registry. Use the legal powers and claims actually established. Land reform requires valid authority, independent adjudication, funding and notice to affected parties. A meeting, survey or political agreement does not transfer ownership by itself.*

The district registrar has brought the public copy of the land map. Several parcels have a question mark beside them. At one table, tenants have brought receipts for the plots they work. Across from them, an estate representative has a deed for one of the same boundaries. The justice officer asks the clerk to keep both copies open while she checks the register.

> **Justice officer:** “We can confirm which deeds are recorded. These receipts don't settle ownership, but they do tell us who says they have been working the land.”

> **Tenant representative:** “We aren't asking you to call it ours today. We want the office to take the claim and tell us what happens next.”

> **Estate representative:** “Then the process should be the same for every holding. There are plots on this map where the register is older than the road.”

The justice officer has costed an independent registry team, legal assistance for claimants, and enough hearings to work through the first set of applications. A separate legal review says some transfers could proceed under current law; others would require new authority. The landowners' association has asked to meet the government before a bill is introduced. The affected residents ask whether their claims will stay on the register if Lara leaves the meeting without a decision.

- **“Fund the registry, legal assistance and independent hearings.”** The justice officer publishes the offices, process and first filing date. Claims enter review; no title changes until an authorized decision is issued. Actual transfers can give households greater legal security and reduce their reliance on local patrons, but the appropriation competes with other programmes and organized land interests may oppose the reform. If the measure lacks current authority or the budget fails, the office returns a written statement of what could not proceed.
- **“Invite named landholders to negotiate voluntary transfers.”** The registrar separates parcels whose owners and boundaries are confirmed. Each affected party receives the same terms and access to legal advice. A transfer takes effect only after valid signatures, required approval and registration. The broker may support a specific agreement in exchange for a defined public role; Lara can accept, reject or counter that bargain. No political promise counts as a transfer.
- **“Keep the existing holdings and use the current local agreements.”** The justice officer keeps the present register. The estate representative offers to continue the services he already controls. Lara can accept the actual service terms if authorized. This preserves immediate local cooperation and likely support from his network; the broker's influence and the claimants' dependence remain, and the land claims are unresolved.
- **“Hold the question until the legal and fiscal review is complete.”** The clerk gives each claimant a receipt and lists which office will return with the missing authority or cost. No title or new service arrangement is implied by the delay. The existing rules remain in force.

If Lara funds the registry or a transfer proceeds, follow with [The first registered transfer](#the-first-registered-transfer) when an actual decision is reportable. If no claim is eligible or the required law and funding are unavailable, report that status and keep existing ownership records in force.

## The coast in season

*Optional Mognagna development scene. Show after a verified access and services review establishes that a coastal visitor programme is feasible. A private offer must identify the land rights and operating terms it can lawfully obtain. Do not trade away public access or presume local consent. Tourism is a separate proposal from emergency relief, the water network, and support for existing livelihoods.*

The municipal meeting is held in a room above the bus office. Through the open window you can hear the ticket clerk calling out the departure time. A local association has brought a calendar of beach clean-ups and weekend markets. Across from them, an investor has set down a plan for a small guesthouse and boat service. The finance officer has left both estimates face down until the parties finish explaining what they need.

> **Association organizer:** “People already come on the weekends. What we don't have is a reliable bus back, or a place to sell food when it rains.”
