# Governing the first term

Writer's brief for the eight-chapter redesign. Read the [budget decisions](../NARRATIVE-CANON.md#two-budget-allocations-during-laras-first-term), [ministry tables](../../gdd/05-mechanics-reference/ministries-and-cabinet/index.md), [regional indices](../../gdd/05-mechanics-reference/indices/index.md) and [existing scene inventory](SCENE-INVENTORY.md). Named GDD programmes, their stated thresholds, the six Great Projects and the author's budget decisions are inherited. Proposed cut examples, fiscal treatment for individual lines, participants and scene variants below are design assignments. Amounts left as letters in the GDD stay open for tuning. Scene headings identify source prose to adapt.

## What two allocations produce

The first five department briefings can share space with existing encounters. Each includes a short, inspectable sheet showing inherited services that can be cut, proposals, implementation period, operating cost afterward and whom the change reaches. The five post-allocation instructions belong to the ministers. Keep a short call short. A minister funded at the inherited level still appears in standing stories. Every programme selected has an owner, date begun, dated effects and an outcome that can be encountered through a scene, local indicator or report.

At the first allocation in chapter 2, favor a small default increase, with the possibility of a small compulsory reduction or a much larger negotiated increase. The Treasury projection includes inherited spending once. After the vote and the debt choice, total departmental allocations must fit available money. Savings from genuine reductions can be moved within or between portfolios. An appropriation or forecast supplies no evidence of delivery. Show separate lines for Great Project payments, debt service and time-limited borrowing.

At the final allocation in chapter 6, carry every funded initiative and any remaining implementation or operating commitments forward. Display released implementation funds only when their payment schedule has actually ended. The free balance can fund a previously skipped initiative, a viable intensification, or another department. Give the player the option of retaining a reserve. The second allocation has no congressional vote and no cancellation menu for a previously funded programme. An inherited service still open to adjustment must be identified individually. Confirm old progress once and show only newly incurred costs or scope. The effects begin from real operation dates. Education and health progress already earned, judicial evidence already received and agreements already signed remain true. Preserve separate sources for public spending, Lara's personal assets and party resources.
