## Design decisions for this draft

Separate a proposal, an agreement, legal authorization and delivery. An agreement attaches to a particular version of a bill, contract or treaty. Material changes require renewed consent from the parties affected. A defeated bill does not cancel existing obligations. Use temporary authority for continuing services where permitted and defer new spending until authorized.

Policy arcs converge at meetings that can acknowledge multiple outcomes. People may know public decisions, their own negotiations, and evidence they have actually received. A local grievance can persist after national indicators improve. Conventions for identifying political support, project eligibility, foreign consent and constitutional procedure remain working design until state and parser integration.

The optional OPP leadership ballot for Livia is a new staged branch. Its party-support threshold, eligibility rules, conference timing, vote conditions, endorsement tracking, her resignation effective date, and the acting Chief of Staff authority are provisional. Lara's endorsement can be recorded as a public statement but cannot determine the party's vote.

A terminal event ends the playable term. At most one terminal ending occurs. Recognition of a Guard intervention is conditional on its contribution to the existing protection design. Electoral outcomes depend on the campaign and governing record. Final office, national outcomes and personal circumstances remain independently authored layers.
