### Worked return card for the post-vote Fontes meeting

The entry gate is an effective congressional outcome or final fallback. A reads signed loan terms, inherited services, expected project payments and spendable room. Maintaining service leaves the principal on its existing schedule. Extra repayment debits available funds and principal by the amount actually paid. Drawing an available loan credits cash and principal, adds service, and gives Lara a finite runway. An offer pending signature can send her back for terms or she may proceed with current funds. Each branch rejoins the allocation folder. Signing writes ministry ceilings once and gives B the authorized choices. Reading the folder later has no fiscal effect. A can test the same signed Health total after a loan and after a transfer from another department: B should offer the same Health choices, and E should inherit different debt and service records.
