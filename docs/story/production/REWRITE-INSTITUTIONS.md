# Institutions, diplomacy and the election

Writer's branch brief for the eight-chapter first term. Use the master calendar for placement. The original scene IDs and section names below identify reusable manuscript material. A return scene needs a new decision, a newly usable opportunity, or an independent actor's answer. Small callbacks belong in existing scenes.

## Authority and handoffs

The [author's narrative canon](../NARRATIVE-CANON.md), including the two allocations, governs the [existing scene inventory](SCENE-INVENTORY.md). First-term story routes and national outcomes below derive from those sources unless identified as proposed. Specific scene pairings, witnesses and suggested player lines are editorial proposals. Preserve the GDD's initiative names and effects. Numerical thresholds, constitutional vote counts, costs and calendar dates remain integration work.

Pass the other writers a short factual record of each relevant decision: the parties involved, the actual terms, the moment it took effect, the evidence held by each party, and the next person entitled to act. A deputy may know about an offer she received. A journalist can ask about the public appointment. Neither can know a private promise simply because the player saw it. Reuse the current officeholder if a minister departed. Show breakdowns in trust through actual changed opportunities, including a demand for performance before a future vote.

## Endings and delivery checks

- The committee gets a scene when material reaches its members through a plausible channel. Grounds and procedural support can produce a hearing. Lara can survive, resign effectively under governing procedure, or be lawfully removed. Private misconduct alone cannot telepathically inform a committee. An independent inquiry can survive Lara's attempt to close an executive review.
- An assassination requires the established threat and institutional conditions. Apply the author's two-survival benchmark, the single survival margin lost upon Jaguar disbandment, and other relevant safeguards. Credit a retained Guard only if its contribution actually changed that outcome, at most once. When Lara survives, return to governing and preserve the reason. A fatality ends playable scenes.
- Military intervention requires an actual, credible rival claim and accumulated institutional conditions. Voss starts opposed to intervention and remains so even if he resigns. A defeated challenge returns to civilian government. Effective removal or confirmed war with Mondrath ends gameplay. Tekoku's loss by itself can change the continuing presidency. Preserve the first-term limit on any definitive regime transformation.
- Deliver one terminal transition. For a regular departure, keep office outcome, personal circumstances and country record independently legible: recognized election or retirement, incurred debt and delivered assets, regional status and living conditions, public institutional findings, and Lara's own family and wealth where established. Unsigned agreements, unspent approvals and campaign claims never become delivered outcomes.
- Before joining manuscripts, trace four routes: a neutral president sustaining equal cooperation with Arenza; a reliable OPP dealmaker whose later inquiry costs the coalition; a president supporting both intelligence tracks after the second allocation; and a retired president who leaves an unresolved case and a completed reform. Also trace an active separatist crisis with peaceful Mondrath and a Strait dispute without separatism. A writer should identify a feasible next scene, its speaker, and a distinct public or private result in every case.
