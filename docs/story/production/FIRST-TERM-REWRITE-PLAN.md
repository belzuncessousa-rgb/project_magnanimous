Preserve approved characterisation, GDD initiatives, and causal history while changing scheduling and encounters. This is the story drafting pass. ### Dramatic structure

| Chapter | Governing decision | What the next writer inherits |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduce Lara in public, cabinet and family settings. GP1 closes the opening with a concrete choice among mine, railway and sanitation. | Actual pledges, port and district terms, project identity. A short tutorial and a pledge-free inaugural address remain viable. |
| 2 | Hear affected ministers and simulate before negotiating. Congress sets spending room. Debt follows the vote. Sign totals with Fontes. Give programme instructions personally. | Support actually obtained, obligations, money available, terms understood by each party, dated mandates. |
| 3 | Put government in contact with a project site, workers, a household or regional office, and foreign counterparts. | People served, requests still outstanding, evidence acquired, consent and permissions. |
| 4 | Confront early results alongside constitutional, tax and labour issues. Return to the public promises and to family. | Effective laws, first verified project phases, public and private knowledge, capacity to respond. |
| 5 | Regional disputes and GP2. Gate the technology hub and extra investment on actual readiness and affordability. | Named project commitments and upcoming instalments enter the next forecast. |
| 6 | Allocation II under existing congressional authority. Give Lara something worth funding late. Preserve the first allocation's commitments. | Additions, operating date, remaining obligations, people now entitled to expect delivery. |
| 7 | Revisit economic choices while Lara still has leverage. Allow institutional conflicts, private life and special routes to resolve through earlier action. | An independently verifiable public record plus unresolved obligations. |
| 8 | Decide candidacy, campaign or retire, resolve eligible terminal routes once, present national and personal outcomes. | A lost election can coexist with national achievement. Term ends on effective transfer. |

Chapter placement and three windows per chapter are editorial planning decisions. Individual projects mature on their own clock. Established GDD timings survive calendar moves. Use [calendar](REWRITE-CALENDAR.md) for specific encounters. Tone belongs to the later editorial pass, but each first draft must already respect the player's ownership of Lara's motives.

### Parallel assignments and ownership

One coordinator owns calendar, shared field dictionary, merges and continuity. Each writer receives a fresh scoped assignment, relevant GDD passages, source scenes and the state rows for those scenes. For shared anthology files submit scene sections separately. The coordinator assembles the anthology. Write under docs/story/rewrite/A through E, one directory per assignment. Work in chapter batches.

| Assignment | Owns | Inputs and outputs |
|---|---|---|
| A • Presidency and fiscal policy | Opening frame, working budget, congressional ceiling, Fontes, debt and both budget documents | Receives D's bloc support and C's capital commitments. Sends signed room, obligation schedule and departmental totals to B. |
| B • Ministries and domestic life | Five minister instructions, delivery of public services, regional economics, port labour, local institutions | Receives A's allocations. Sends mandates and operational dates to D, regional conditions to C/E. B writes Kesht's instruction. D writes cases. |
| C • Projects and diplomacy | Both project rounds, site terms, procurement, delivery, lenders and external diplomacy | Receives A's resources, B's regional readiness, D's attributable evidence. Returns signed deals, dated phases, consent and counterpart commitments. |
| D • Political institutions | Port appointment bargain, PEC, tax enactment, party politics, investigations, extraordinary endings | Supplies support, enacted measures, verified findings, officeholder transitions. A owns budget vote prose. B owns service delivery. |
| E • Personal life and election | Family, Amanda, personal finances, optional free actions, candidacy, speeches and epilogues | Receives publicly known outcomes, private relationship facts, lawful candidacy and ending eligibility. Owns final presentation. |

### Prompt for the coordinator

> Use main as the source of truth. Read FIRST-TERM-REWRITE-PLAN.md and SCENE-STATE-HANDOFF.md in docs/story/production. Run assignments A through E with parallel subagents and fresh, task-specific context. Give each writer only their chapter range, scene names, relevant canon and GDD passages, prose samples, and incoming/outgoing state. First draft the connected sequence from the port agreement through the budget and minister instructions to the first consequence. Trace a contrasting pair of routes across the writers. Reconcile shared fields, then continue chapter by chapter through the full term. Keep changes on a rewrite branch for review. Return one integrated manuscript and route report. Escalate only a genuine canon conflict that would change an approved design.

### Acceptance routes

1. Quiet Lara skips optional promises, keeps Health flat and chooses foreign shielding on unchanged Intelligence. Existing care and crisis access continue. Families know only what she told them.
2. Congress delivers a contraction. Lara protects one inherited service by making a named reduction elsewhere. Both budgets and handover remain reachable.
3. Clean coalition organizing and a patronage concession win equal totals. Services agree, but later access to the port director and political obligations differ. Neither route establishes illicit financing on its own.
4. Compare early and late institute starts at an eligible host. Regional effects reflect elapsed time. Its twelve-turn GDP payoff never fires at signature.
5. Compare deep foreign shielding, split Intelligence and a late domestic track. No retroactive leads, double deep access or cancellation of an existing track.
6. Compare finite borrowing and accelerated repayment. Debt and assets persist after electoral defeat. An unsigned agreement never supplies cash.
7. Compare mine with limited consent, railway with an unopened stop, and sanitation in a district outside Celia's street. Region-wide averages never establish delivery to a specific household.
8. Compare retirement, certified loss and an effective exceptional ending. Candidate-only scenes require registration; effective transfer happens once. E closes on personal circumstances and actual public results.

After the pilot, coordinator verifies the producer and consumer of every newly shared fact. After assembly read the quiet, transactional and institution-building routes for character voice and fatigue. Engine integration separately verifies eligibility, fiscal balance and dispatch.
