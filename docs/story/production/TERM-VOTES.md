### Accept the costed amendment

### Release the slot

Livia calls the clerk to release the scheduled slot. Mertens waits until she ends the call.

> **Mertens:** “Tell me when I can show them a new version. I can ask for another meeting. I'd like to be able to say what we're meeting about.”

- **“Leave the bill deferred.”** Livia records that no new vote date has been requested. The bill does not pass or fail; its current commitments remain subject to the authority already in force.

## The first day at the desk

*Conditional report after a costed filing-assistance service was approved and the named offices confirmed staffing. Use only the records actually returned; never invent a count of callers or completed filings.*

The Revenue clerk sets the office schedule beside a receipt from one of the listed return desks. She has marked the date that the extra hours began.

> **Clerk:** “This desk opened on the date we agreed. They have a place to bring the forms and someone assigned to answer questions.”

Fontes checks the schedule against the notice. “The service helps people file. It doesn't change what they owe or when it is due.”

*If Lara previously requested a review, and Revenue has enough verified records:* The clerk returns with the dates and the offices' combined service totals. - **“Keep the current schedule and review it at the next budget meeting.”** The clerk files the report. This does not renew the service beyond its present authorization.
