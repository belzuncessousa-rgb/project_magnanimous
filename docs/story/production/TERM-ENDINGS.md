# Election and handover

*Routing. A confirmed terminal event takes precedence over the ordinary calendar. If two events become eligible together, resolve them in the order they actually take effect. Once an event ends Lara's tenure or life, subsequent events cannot end it again. If a hearing opens, a negotiation becomes urgent, or an attempt is survived, resolve that interruption and return to the calendar. During a campaign, do not award an election outcome before the certified result or the final resolution of a viable challenge.*

| Established result | Closing route |
|---|---|
| Lara is certified to return for a new term | Reelected |
| Another candidate is certified after Lara contested the election | Defeated |
| Lara chose not to run, her nomination failed before registration, or the commission accepted her withdrawal before ballot certification | Handover, with the appropriate retirement or nomination context |
| A removal decision takes effect or Lara resigns during the proceeding | Hearing handover |
| An attempt is fatal under the protection rules | Public broadcast and eligible memorial |
| Civilian authority fails and military removal takes effect | Order from command |
| The Mondrath conflict reaches the terminal outbreak | Announcement from the strait |

*Personal passages that require Lara to speak or act need her to be alive and able to make that choice. After the terminal scene, read the closing passages in the order below. They cannot advance the calendar or reopen a resolved end.*

| Closing route | Eligible material after the scene |
|---|---|
| Reelected | National closing passages based on the first-term record, then the new-term diary as the final image. Lara is still in office, so skip the departure and post-office work scenes. |
| Defeated or ordinary handover | National closing passages based on the first-term record, followed by Lara's choice of an available next step. Staying and leaving are mutually exclusive. Family and Amanda can appear if their circumstances permit. |
| Hearing handover | National closing passages based on the record at departure. Use a personal scene only if Lara has the freedom to take that step. An unresolved proceeding remains in the public record wherever disclosure is permitted. |
| Fatal attack | Public broadcast and eligible memorial. No later choice from Lara. A national passage may report an established condition from before her death, without attributing later work to her. |
| Military removal | Public account of the actual transfer or dispute. A personal scene requires Lara's situation to be established and the offered action to be feasible. |
| War outbreak | Use the verified notices at the outbreak and close. Omit routine aftermath that depends on services or decisions beyond that point. |

*National passages can accompany distinct sectors of the same result. Within a sector, select the variants that match each actual project, service or jurisdiction. Do not show the same phase as both open and still awaiting its first opening. An available room, employer, or companion matters only if the preceding route establishes it or the epilogue introduces it credibly.*
