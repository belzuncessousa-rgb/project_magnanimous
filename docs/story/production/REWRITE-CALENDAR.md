# Eight-chapter scene rewrite calendar

This working calendar covers four years over 24 playable turns. The first and final ministerial allocations fall in Chapters 2 and 6. The two Great Project selections fall in Chapters 1 and 5. The election and normal handover belong in Chapter 8. Scene placement is editorial staging. The GDD and narrative canon determine programme availability, cost, prerequisites, political authority and consequences. Retain existing scene IDs when moving them. Date existing prototypes and popups from actual events during integration.

Use [the existing inventory](SCENE-INVENTORY.md) for the manuscript and [the narrative canon](../NARRATIVE-CANON.md) for author decisions. The timetable below assigns existing scenes and specifies the new work. A turn is a window for decisions. Only eligible regional proposals, offers, crises and family calls appear. A turn can advance if a contingent proposal never materializes. A separate, later result linked to the same project requires a distinct phase. A declined or expired invitation cannot fulfil an unrelated prerequisite.

## Dispatch and editing checks

- On a quiet route, the player can still reach both budgets, both Great Project decisions and a normal ending. If political support is weak, the first budget still resolves to a known spendable amount; a failed constitutional package leaves other governing choices open.
- Retain appropriate standing, seasonal, conditional and personal free actions. Free actions can add evidence, relationships, capacity or progress. The office has to actually receive information before its holder uses it. Share public facts through newspapers, radio, readable notices, social posts and residents in the affected region.
- A signed commitment, actual payment, operating service and policy effect each have their own timing. Payoffs name a person, location, budget, lender, region or office that can experience the change. Count GDP milestones from actual implementation. A single real-world outcome can change several indices without becoming several scenes.
- Run the 24-turn calendar against chapter resource refreshes, monthly income, debt servicing, age and season references. Use relative timing where plausible. Do not copy the former eighteen-turn budget or chapter numbers into dialogue or triggers.
- Interrupt ordinary business only when a qualifying terminal event takes effect. Nonterminal hearings, elections abroad, project delays and cabinet changes return to pending business with the valid officeholder and outstanding commitments.
