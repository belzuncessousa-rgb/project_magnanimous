# The revised budget

Staged scene after the district-allocation talks. Treasury has costed the proposal. Where Lara deferred, Treasury has costed an option for her to consider. Mertens can negotiate for the OPP. The rewrite requires a separate confirmation of support before the vote.

When you arrive, Mertens is standing at the cabinet table, reading the budget with his coat still on. Fontes has opened the papers at the page on district investment.

> **Mertens:** “Can our deputies submit requests under this line?”

> **Fontes:** “I was waiting for someone to ask me how large the line could be.”

Mertens takes off his coat and hangs it over the back of a chair.

> **Mertens:** “Well, now you know what I was going to ask after that.”

### Published allocations

> **Mertens:** “I can ask our bench to support a common fund. They'll want their requests considered on the same terms as Pelan's.”

> **Lara:** “Are they asking for the same amount?”

> **Mertens:** “Some are. I think I can get them to ask for the same rules.”

Fontes slides the eligibility sheet towards him.

> **Fontes:** “Then take them this page. It tells them which requests the ministries can actually fund.”

- **“Offer both benches the same published rules.”** Mertens takes the sheet. “I'll start there. Let Pelan see the same copy before I go.”
- **“Keep the rules as drafted. See who will support them.”** Mertens looks at the sheet again. “All right. I'll tell you where the objections are before anyone promises you votes.”

### Private allocations

Mertens takes a folded sheet out of his inside pocket. There are names beside some of the proposed works.

> **Mertens:** “I asked our deputies for the requests they'd want to discuss first. One of them has put a bridge on here twice.”

> **Fontes:** “Two bridges?”

> **Mertens:** “One bridge. Two deputies.”

He shows Fontes the entries.

> **Mertens:** “The mayors on either side have both asked for it. We'll need to settle who puts in the request.”

- **“Bring both benches' requests to me together.”** Mertens hands the sheet to Fontes. “Get Pelan's list too. If the bridge is on his, I'd like to know before I make another phone call.”
- **“Keep our bench's arrangement. I won't offer the OPP a share.”** Mertens folds the sheet again. “I'll tell them. If you want me to ask them to support the rest of the bill, I'll need to know what I can discuss with them.”

### National investment

> **Mertens:** “There's a deputy on our bench who keeps asking about the railway. She'd like to tell her district when the route decision is due.”

Fontes looks up.

> **Fontes:** “Do we have a railway in this bill?”

> **Mertens:** “I told her I'd find out. She hasn't seen this schedule yet.”

He reaches for the investment schedule.

- **“Take them the actual investment schedule.”** Mertens turns to the first page. “I'll take Planning with me. If someone asks about their district, I want an answer they can repeat at home.”
- **“Ask for support on the bill as it stands.”** He leaves the papers on the table. “I'll ask. Some of them will want to explain their vote before they give it to us.”

## before-the-vote | Before the vote

Fontes brings the expenditure summary back to the top of the pile.

> **Fontes:** “When you've both finished, I need the version that goes to Congress. If we put anything else on this page, I need to know where the money comes from.”

- **“Circulate the revised bill.”** Fontes puts the dated copy on top. “Send them this one. If a page changes, I want both the old page and the replacement back before we print it again.” Take the confirmed version to the floor scene.
- **“Hold it for the revised figures.”** Mertens puts on his coat. “I'll ask them to keep the meeting open. Give me a time to come back before I tell them which day.” Hold the vote until there is a complete text, or close this attempt as deferred.
