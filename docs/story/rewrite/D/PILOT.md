# D · Port appointments and district terms

Staged manuscript for chapters 1–2. The officeholder names are provisional. The authority's dated record governs appointments.

## 1.1 · Counting Votes

Mertens is pouring coffee when you arrive. Livia hands you a sheet with the parties in two columns.

> **Livia:** “One hundred seats. Fifty-one for an ordinary bill.”
>
> **Mertens:** “We have twenty-four. My party has twenty, and the Communists have six.”
>
> **Lara:** “That's fifty.”
>
> **Mertens:** “If the Communists like the bill.”

The Capital Party has seventeen, the National Front twelve, Faith and Family nine, the Civic Alliance eight. Four names are listed separately. Livia lays a second sheet beside the first.

> **Livia:** “Salgado said this morning that the port unions wanted to see us. There's also a vacancy at the authority.”
>
> **Mertens:** “Odel has been asking about that since the election.”
>
> **Livia:** “He wants to put forward a director and about forty supervisors.”
>
> **Mertens:** “He started at sixty.”
>
> **Livia:** “Fifty-two.”
>
> **Mertens:** “I'm glad one of us wrote it down.”

You recognize two department names from the investigation that brought down the last government.

Ask any of these once, or decide. The existing party questions can precede the port discussion.

- **“What does Odel expect us to give him?”** Mertens taps the director's line. “A date he can take back to his people. Forty applications will keep them busy. The date is what he'll ask me about first.”
- **“What can he bring us on the budget?”** Mertens sets his cup down. “If the terms suit them, I can ask Mantel to put the text to the bench. I'll tell you who says yes.”
- **“What happens if the candidates don't meet the requirements?”** Livia turns the page over. “The authority returns those applications. I'll have them put the reasons in writing.”
- **“I promised the appointments would be on merit.”** If Lara made a public integrity pledge covering appointments, Livia says, “I'll ask the authority to keep the assessments. The newspapers will have your speech in front of them when the names come out.”

### Set the terms

1. **“Run an ordinary merit search for all the posts.”** Livia puts Odel's sheet back in the folder. “The authority can issue the call this week.” Mertens picks up the seat list. “Then I'd like a word with Telves before he leaves.”
2. **“Consider Odel's nominees for the other posts. Keep the director independent.”** Livia draws a line under the director's post. “I'll ask for names for these. The authority will assess each application.”
3. **“Let Odel put forward a director as well.”** Mertens takes out his phone. “I'll ask whether his candidate can come in this week.” Livia keeps her hand on the sheet. “I'll have the authority check the file and tell us which decisions it can make.” If Lara asks for the checks in writing, Livia requests the signed assessment for each post. Mertens asks what he can take back to Odel.
   - **“If she clears review, I’ll put her appointment forward for confirmation.”** Mertens repeats the condition back. “Then I’ll tell him what you’ve agreed to. We can discuss the bill once she’s in office.” Record the specific conditional appointment undertaking. This route offers supervisors individual consideration, subject to qualification.
   - **“I’m offering her an assessment.”** Mertens says, “I’ll tell him to send her application.” Keep the selection open under the authority’s process.
4. **“Decline Odel's request.”** Mertens folds his copy. “Is that the port, or any office I go back to him with?” Lara may set a general redline. Livia asks the authority to use its ordinary process for vacancies.
5. **“Hold the talks until we have a budget draft.”** Livia dates the sheet and puts it away. Mertens says, “When we have a text, I'll ask if Odel still wants to talk.”

## 1.3 · The Bench

Avros opens a folder of district requests at a photograph of two padlocked classroom doors.

> **Avros:** “The municipality sent him to Education. Education sent him back. He'd like to go home with a department and a date.”

- **“Ask Education for a decision through its ordinary review.”** Avros writes the ministry's name on the photograph sleeve. “I'll tell him they'll assess it with the other repairs.” An adverse decision can satisfy these terms.
- **“Bring me a cost and an eligible funding line for these classrooms. I'll take the repair forward if both clear.”** Avros slips the photograph into a smaller folder. “I'll ask the municipality for something the ministry can use.” The commitment attaches to this repair. The actionable estimate and funding decision can remain pending.
- **“Bring it back with the district requests when the budget is ready.”** Avros closes the folder. “I'll ask him to send whatever he has by then.”

Avros turns to the larger district list. “The others will ask whether they can send in their own proposals.”

- **“Open a published district process.”** Avros asks which office will publish the allocations.
- **“Let the deputies negotiate a private list.”** Avros puts the list back in the folder and asks you who should receive a copy.
- **“Prioritize the national programme.”** Avros leaves the repair photograph with the individual applications.
- **“Return to the district terms at budget time.”** Avros asks for the date of the next meeting.

These terms govern talks. A district appropriation, submitted version and funding decision each follow on their own record.

- **Read** Actual request, Education jurisdiction, existing district terms.

Record deputy, school scope, estimate status, approval conditions and what Avros was asked to convey. Keep `avros_school_response` and `district_terms_response` for the later bench conversation where applicable.

## 2.1 · Names for the Port

*When the authority has a shortlist or reviewed nominations. Otherwise the appointments stay pending and the budget discussion proceeds with the commitments it actually has.*

Livia has left the applications in a stack. She moves the director's page to the top.

> **Livia:** “The authority has finished its first checks. This page is for the director. I can send the other decisions separately.”

*Merit or supervisory nominees only.* The director's shortlist came through the ordinary search. Marta Leal's assessment lists her term running the freight terminal. On the supervisory nomination route, any eligible party applicants for the other posts appear on separate pages.

*Directorship nomination.* Irena Vale's file lists her years managing the eastern docks. Mertens rests a finger beside her name.

> **Mertens:** “Odel asked whether she should be there Monday.”
>
> **Livia:** “I'll tell him when we have the signed date.”

- **“Send the cleared appointments for confirmation.”** Livia takes the eligible pages within the agreed scope. When the authority's signed record comes back, she lays it in front of you. “Leal takes office Monday.” On the nomination route, use Vale when the required approvals return. If an approval is outstanding, Livia shows you the missing entry and gives the next review date if known.
- **“Return the applications with a reason.”** Available for an identified issue. Livia attaches the authority's findings. On actual rejection the affected nominee receives the decision. Eligible remaining files may proceed within their terms.
- **“Hold the appointments.”** Livia dates the instruction. “The acting director can keep the shifts covered. I'll ask when the authority needs a decision about the vacancy.”

*On the actual effective date, if Vale fulfils the agreed term and Mertens is willing to undertake the work, he brings Odel’s acknowledgment.*

> **Mertens:** “She took office this morning. Give me the bill when you have it and I’ll take it to Mantel.”

*Record the undertaking to circulate. A’s bench meeting sends the dated version. The list at the door gets the actual replies after circulation.*

**Branch card**

- **Read** Offer, applications, jurisdiction, eligibility, lawful confirmation record and effective date. The whip report reads the current ceiling version and actual bench replies.
- **Choice** Send cleared files, return problem files, or hold.
- **Write** Per post, pending, confirmed or rejected. Record confirmed appointee and effective date, and delivery of an OPP term only when fulfilled. Hand A the undertaking when made. D supplies any later version-specific OPP replies to A after the text is circulated.
- **Knowledge** Authority and applicants receive their decisions. Public callbacks use released appointment facts. Private terms and whip replies travel through their recipients.
- **Next / fallback** Material changes require renewed support. Later labour talks use the effective director or sitting authority and hear the union delegate independently. Either director can reach a settlement or fail.
