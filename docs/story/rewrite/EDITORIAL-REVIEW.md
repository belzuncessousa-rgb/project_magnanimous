# First-term editorial review

First pass on the pilot and selected later scenes in the [first-term manuscript](FIRST-TERM-MANUSCRIPT.md). This review measures the experience of reading and making choices. The timing targets come from the author. The scene treatments below are editorial proposals.

## Review standard

- **Speech throughout the manuscript.** The Congress line is a diagnostic. Read the exchange aloud and ask what this person would naturally say to this listener. Livia can speak in full sentences without losing precision. Lara's “That's fifty” works as an immediate reaction. Save clipped speech for an intentional voice, moment or change in pressure. Read characters across adjacent scenes for repeated cadences, identical sentence shapes and replies that sound like summaries of their own dialogue.
- **Time on one route.** For a scene like Counting Votes, aim for about ten minutes even if the player skips optional topics, with intermediate paths and roughly thirty minutes for a curious player. Other major scenes generally aim for at least fifteen minutes on an engaged route, with roughly three major scenes per turn as a planning target. Minor encounters can be brief and still have room for an opening, a response and some aftermath. Count only the text and choices a player actually sees. Time a read-through before claiming a draft meets the target.
- **Conversation with a purpose and room to wander.** A player should be able to stay on a topic, change it or leave it alone. Let speakers disagree, miss a point, tell a story, or answer a nearby question. Give Lara low-stakes ways to participate, including greetings and recurring habits. Those choices can stay expressive. Achievements for repeated habits are a separate design possibility.
- **Consequential moments.** A major scene can have multiple commitments. Give each its own setup and confirmation. Put indispensable decisions on the route everyone sees, or let the player explicitly defer or delegate them where that makes sense. An optional exchange that can affect a relationship or indicator needs a visible reason to engage. If declining closes an opportunity, show what Lara is passing up. Avoid a blanket penalty for reading fewer topics.
- **Advice under uncertainty.** Show firsthand knowledge, informed guesses and genuine unknowns in the conversation. Competent advisers can misjudge what someone will demand. Count actual votes from individuals or defensible groups when the bill reaches that point. Early political advice can point to an opening without booking support.

### Counting Votes route tests for the next draft

1. **Direct route, about ten minutes.** Keep the arrival, working relationship, political exposure, Odel's ask, credible alternatives and what Lara is instructing Mertens to do. Lara reaches the decision with the essentials even if she declines every optional topic.
2. **Selective route.** Follow one or two subjects. For instance, ask about the Capital Party, challenge the road assumption and hear what Livia actually knows. Then ask what a refusal would cost Mertens before deciding. Each subject should run as a conversation with natural exits.
3. **Curious route, up to about thirty minutes.** Follow disagreement across several topics, revisit advice when a new detail warrants it and make some expressive choices. Give the player new material on the later topics so the longer route does more than repeat the stakes.

The current Counting Votes section has roughly 620 words even with every branch and instruction included. Any one route sees less. The rewrite needs additional scenes within the meeting: people interrupting and responding, specific political uncertainties, reactions to Lara's position and room to hear each other out. The length of a branch inventory cannot establish play time.

## Prose revision and verification

This revision edits all 78 scheduled scenes in the full manuscript, plus the residence money material and the four conditional interruptions. The 19 shared pilot scenes use the same revised prose. The two manuscript files are drafts for the narrative layer. The A–E files retain their use as source and handoff packets.

Counting Votes puts political exposure and the alternatives to Odel on every route. Optional questions distinguish an adviser's informed guess from what a deputy actually said. The budget vote now gives the player separate conversations about the school undertaking, the version the benches saw, and the practical fallback before the clerk is asked to proceed. The later scenes have additions specific to their cast and situation.

The author's dense-budget pacing targets are roughly 25–30 minutes on the shortest route and around 40 minutes for a fuller one. The manuscript has no timing telemetry. It needs single-route reads to measure these targets, and the current text alone does not establish that they are met. Some scenes have a short new exchange and remain shorter than the major encounters.
