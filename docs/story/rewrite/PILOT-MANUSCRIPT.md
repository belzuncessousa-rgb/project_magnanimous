### 3.1 · The Hours at Sugar Bay

**Operating.** A nurse opens the second consulting room. Reception calls the next name from the new follow-up list. At the desk the woman from the first visit sets down a folded bus timetable. The clerk writes the date of her son’s next visit on a card.

> **Woman** — “Wednesday the ninth?”
>
> **Clerk** — “At three. Come to this desk first.”
>
> **Dara** — “Both afternoon sessions are staffed now. She can book around the bus.”

**Current hours.** If present for a standing visit, the woman asks for an afternoon appointment. The clerk checks the existing book and offers its next morning slot. Dara confirms payment renewals separately if effective. Otherwise proceed to the next scene without the service report.

**Pending.** If asked about the instruction, the director leaves the current rota on the glass. “The cover still needs confirmation. I can give her a morning slot today.” Give the next check if scheduled.

**Reduced.** Following a verified closure, the clerk takes down the closed block, calls the woman and confirms the new date and location. If contact fails, show the outstanding call. Count bookings and visits separately.

When a service follow-up takes place Lara can **“Confirm the dates with patients,” “Cost another session,”** or **“Report the next change.”** Dara takes the corresponding notice, estimates request or report schedule.
