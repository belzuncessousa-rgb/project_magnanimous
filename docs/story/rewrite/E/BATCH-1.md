# E · Family, residence and personal accounts

## 3.2 · Sunday in Vila Tamarin

*Optional, at vila-tamarin. The invitation is a family visit. When it can be taken, show the diary time before confirming. The street notice can be read here or on a separate visit when the signed report arrives.*

Your father pulls a chair out of the sun with his foot. He has spread the Sunday paper over the other two.

> **Father:** “Your mother says she told you what happened at church.”
>
> **Celia:** “I said I would tell her when she got here.”
>
> **Lara:** “What happened?”
>
> **Celia:** “I got dizzy and sat down. Ivone walked me home. I called the clinic the next morning. They gave me a time.”

She puts a jug on the table. Your father lifts the sports page so she can set down the glasses.

- **“Ask the clinic about a cancellation?”** Celia agrees to a call about an ordinary opening. “If there's one in the morning, ask how early. I need to see who can take me.” Send the inquiry only after Lara confirms. Bring back the answer and any appointment Celia actually attends separately.
- **“You could come stay with me.”** Celia looks at your father. “I'll keep the appointment here. I might come to see you another time.” Record her answer to this offer.
- **“Let me know how it goes.”** “I will. Your father has written it on the calendar twice.”

### The newsletter

Celia fetches the last issue from the windowsill. A photograph of the church kitchen takes up half a page. She points to the woman beside the stove.

> **Celia:** “That's the apron your sister gave me. She said it was too good to use.”
>
> **Father:** “It was white when she gave it to you.”
>
> **Celia:** “Ezequiel asked if I'd sit for a picture with him next week. He wants to put it here.”

If Amanda is accompanying Lara, she recognizes the layout. “I've seen the party office copy pages from that newsletter.” Otherwise Lara can ask Celia where it goes. “There are copies at the church. After that I couldn't tell you.”

- **“What would you like to do?”** Celia reads the caption again. “I think I'll sit for it. They helped me get meals out on Wednesdays when I had nobody to drive.”
- **“Would you ask him to keep it in the newsletter?”** Celia agrees to sit and asks Ezequiel to exclude party use. “I'll tell him before we take it. He'll want to know why, so I'll tell him that too.”
- **“I'd rather you let this one pass.”** Celia taps the page. “I'll tell him myself. I still have to ask him for the kitchen key.”

Then offer **“Tell him I'll come to a service”** or **Continue with the visit**. If Lara promises, Celia says, “I'll ask him which Sunday he's there.” Celia's own decision about the picture stands. Only a newsletter issue that actually appears can supply E's published photo. A request about reuse travels with the photo only to recipients who have actually received the request.

### The address on the notice

*Offer when the signed notice covers this street. If it has yet to arrive, Lara can ask about the association meeting or check on her earlier information promise with Planning. If she made a personal guarantee, Celia may ask what she should tell the association. Answer from the record and keep the guarantee open until its terms are met.*

Your father holds down the corner of the map with his glass. Celia reads the house numbers while Lara follows them down the page. Use the exact limits, dates and responsible office from the notice.

| Notice received | At the table |
|---|---|
| Assessment | Celia puts her finger at the bend. “So they know where they'd dig. Who decides whether they can pay for it?” |
| Approved construction commitment | Your father turns the notice over to check for a works date. “It gives the office and the review. I'll write both down for Thursday.” |
| Works complete, service awaiting opening | Celia looks across at the houses on the bend. “People can see where they filled the trench. Who's going to tell them when they can use it?” |

- **Ask for the next recorded decision or household check.** Send the question for this address to the office on the notice. Return with its actual answer or leave the question open.
- **Ask for the estimate and funding requirements.** Offer when funding is unresolved. Celia writes the office's name on the back of the association letter.
- **Leave the notice with the association.** Celia makes a copy if she agrees to share it. Any money she set aside remains her decision.

### Heading back

At the gate your father asks how long it takes to drive back. Celia asks him to hold the gate while she brings out a bag of rolls.

- **Stay for dinner.** When there is time, your father brings the chairs in. Celia wants to know whether Lara's sister has found anyone to look at the noise in her car.
- **“I'll call you on Tuesday.”** Use an available time Celia agrees to. She says, “After supper. I leave the phone in the other room while I'm cooking.”
- **Take the rolls and say goodbye.**

## 4.2 · Katanaí General

*Optional interrupt after confirmed admission and permitted notification. A dizzy spell, a missed call and a clinic appointment each have their own record. If there is no admission, the usual family contact can still be available.*

Amanda calls before your first appointment.

> **Amanda:** “Your father rang the residence. He's with your mother at Katanaí General. He says she's awake. He asked me to tell you.”

- **Go during visiting hours.** Show the diary time required. At the ward your father moves his bag off the visitor's chair. Celia says, “Put it back under the bed. Every time someone comes in he thinks we're leaving.”
- **Call the ward.** If Celia can take the call, she asks you to wait while she puts her glasses down. “They want me to stay until they've read everything. Could you tell your father where he put my cardigan?” Lara can ask for visiting hours or agree on another call.
- **Ask Dad to ring when there is news.** Amanda gives him the direct number when he can be reached. Any reply comes from him.

### At the bedside

- **Take her hand.** She moves the television remote to her other hand.
- **Ask what the doctors have said.** Your father starts to read the visiting sheet. Celia points him to the page they were given that morning.
- **“How did it go with the clinic?”** Show only if the inquiry was made and Celia has a result she can share. She describes the visit, the lack of a cancellation or the offered time she couldn't get to. If no result is available, leave the exchange out.

With Celia's permission, the doctor says she is stable and the team still has assessments to complete. She asks where Celia would like to continue her care. Celia says she would rather go home when the team clears her to leave.

- **Ask for ordinary follow-up.** The doctor checks what the local clinic can provide. A referral or a booking can be reported at its actual stage.
- **Offer to pay for care Celia chooses.** Celia asks to see what the care involves and what it would cost. An accepted arrangement needs a provider, her consent and confirmed terms. Use the personal expense action on an actual quote. A payment clears separately.
- **Offer both parents two weeks at the residence.** Your father asks whether he can get to the house from there on his own. Celia says they'll talk it over. Open the residence discussion.
- **Ask Dara about seeing a specialist sooner.** In the corridor, Dara asks, “Do you want me to check the normal openings, or ask who'd have to wait if they put her first?” Choose **Use the ordinary process**, **Ask for priority even if someone waits longer**, or **Leave it with the ward**. A priority request goes for a separate capacity and patient-impact response through the people who can decide. Report only a shareable decision and any confirmed appointment or actual change to the queue.

Amanda asks when you want the car. **Stay until visiting ends**, when the diary allows, or **Return to work**. If you stay, Celia asks you to find the channel with the afternoon comedy. Your father goes home for a clean shirt.

On either connected contact Lara can offer **“I'll call tomorrow”** and agree a time. When the time comes, present a ward call if she remains admitted and can take it, or the home route if she's been discharged. Offer ordinary contact on its own availability as well. On connection Celia can talk about how she's doing. In the ward she can share a confirmed update time if she has one. At home she asks whether Lara has had supper. A missed time leaves a missed promise if Lara lets the agreed opportunity pass. If Celia is unavailable, leave a message or agree another time when possible.

### The service time

*Optional separate appearance after the Sunday promise and a confirmed time from Ezequiel. Celia attends only if independently able and willing.*

A volunteer carries cups out to the hall. Ezequiel moves along the bench so you can sit.

> **Ezequiel:** “The woman who's reading today asked whether she could bring her boy. That's him by the door. You can sit anywhere he hasn't left his coat.”

- **Stay for the service.** Afterwards choose tea with the volunteers or say goodbye. At tea a volunteer asks Ezequiel if the kitchen can keep the large pot until Thursday. He goes to check with the person who lent it.
- **Ask for another Sunday.** Keep the promise open while Ezequiel checks the next time.
- **Withdraw the promise.** Lara can tell Celia on the next call or ask Ezequiel to pass on the message. Record who actually tells her.

## 4.3 · A Quiet Evening

*Optional at the four-year midpoint, at the residence. The cabinet review remains in its own slot.*

Mertens has a page folded in his breast pocket. He takes it out and puts it on the sitting-room table.

> **Mertens:** “Things Congress will ask about when it gets back. Livia put dates on her copy. You should probably use hers.”

Choose **Ask about his break** or **Say goodnight**. If asked, he says he's visiting family. “My wife asked me to leave the party telephone here. I told her I'd see if the battery lasts another four years first.” He puts the telephone into his pocket and asks Amanda if she'd like him to leave the page.

Amanda brings the family correspondence and a thin folder from her bag. **Read one of the letters**, **Check a recorded promise**, or **Leave the papers for tomorrow**. Letters can be answered personally or referred to the appropriate office for a reply. A promise page opens only for terms Lara actually gave. On a public promise, use the responsible office's dated public or shareable record. If a decision remains, ask for the date it can be confirmed, or leave the file as it stands. **Include the dated status in a public update** is available for publishable material only. A street assurance can be reviewed against the signed address notice. Private care, calls and expenses belong with their respective correspondence.

Amanda leaves a photograph by the lamp. Two rows of former law students are lined up against the university wall. Someone on the end has stepped forward to let another person into the picture.

> **Amanda:** “There's Clara. She was the one who always brought the cases we'd forgotten to read.”

If they previously discussed the borrowed jacket, she points it out. “I thought it fit for that whole afternoon.”

Choose **Ask who she still hears from**, **Suggest getting together again**, or **Look at the photograph with her**. If Lara suggests a reunion, Amanda says she'll see which numbers she still has. At the door she asks whether she should leave the photograph. Lara can keep it or ask her to take it home. The evening closes independently of family or political outcomes.

## Between turns · Residence and personal money

**Two weeks at the residence.** Return after the offer when both parents can answer. Your father asks how he'd get home to collect the post. Celia wants the clinic to confirm the follow-up route. **Confirm the dates** only with both parents' agreement, a suitable room and feasible care. Record a booked stay on confirmation and its start when they arrive. **Keep talking** leaves outstanding arrangements open. **Withdraw the offer** closes that offer. At departure, Celia asks where your father has left the spare key.

**The declaration.** The financial officer places a copy of the last filing beside the entries for salary actually received. If a permitted sale or investment is available, show the restrictions, disclosure and personal cost before Lara decides. **Keep the current holdings** or **Approve the transaction on these terms**. Return on completion with the actual settlement or loss. When material changes require it, return to the officer for a revised declaration.

**Pay an expense.** A consenting relative brings a quote, or a registered local group brings the outstanding balance on an order. Show the amount, available personal funds and any disclosure before confirming. **Cover it**, **Offer part**, or **Leave the money where it is**. On partial help the recipient decides whether they can proceed. Use the same payment distinction for a permitted, disclosed campaign expense once there is a candidacy and an authorized invoice. Only cleared payments produce receipts. A promise to help can remain open.

**A benefit tied to a public decision.** Offer when an actual approach occurs and Lara hears its terms. **Decline**, **Refer the documented offer for review**, or **Accept the offered terms**, when story conditions permit and the impropriety is made clear. Record accessible funds only if the benefit arrives. Keep the source, what was requested and who received evidence as distinct facts.
