## 3.1 · The first work order

*Sources: [Who Builds It](../../production/p2-contractor.md), [Arennian Ambassador](../../production/p2-pryce.md), [Zharani Envoy](../../production/p2-sadrah.md), [rail](../../production/p2-site-rail.md), [Tamarin](../../production/p2-site-tamarin.md), [Tauá](../../production/p2-site-taua.md).*

Marne has taken the glass cover off the model. He asks Fontes to put the working folder beside it, clear of the little trees.

> **Marne:** “This is the part we were asked to price.”
>
> **Fontes:** “And this is what we could actually release. I've taken out the money we already owe.”

If the balance is insufficient, Fontes shows the smallest independently viable scope he can cost or strikes the proposed start. A prior referral for independent estimates, a legal opinion or a publication undertaking comes forward when the office received it. If Lara already received the Sugar Bay report, Marne can acknowledge the verified clinic hours only if Dara passed on that result. Keep the visit and the works decision on their own records.

**Procurement.** For the mine, **“Hear the domestic consortium,” “Seek terms from Halvard Minerals,”** or **“Seek the Zharani partnership”** requests terms with the necessary expertise, ownership, purchase commitments and access obligations. For rail, **“Ask the domestic firms,” “Open an international tender,”** or **“Require domestic participation”** sets the solicitation, including what local firms must supply. For sanitation, **“Use existing terms if counsel clears them,” “Renegotiate with Corvel,”** or **“Prepare to terminate and tender the remainder”** requests a contract route. Corvel's inherited obligations appear once and can survive retendering. **“Have the terms independently checked”** or **“Prepare the documents for publication”** routes the papers to staff. Publication is fulfilled when the releasable documents go out.

**Foreign approaches.** Play the open contacts separately. Pryce leaves his hat in the waiting room. Sadrah brings a schedule with one column for local orders and one for imported equipment.

> **Pryce:** “The Federation can bring firms to the table. Our people will ask whether Zharan can bid for the same strategic orders.”
>
> **Lara:** “Which orders are you talking about?”
>
> **Pryce:** “I'll have them identified on the paper.”
>
> **Sadrah:** “We can give the bank a figure sooner if we know who will supply the equipment. If you want to keep an order open, I'll ask them to price it separately.”

Optional **“Ask about Tarev cooperation”** appears with a pertinent offer or shareable D evidence. Aroun uses only the attributable finding cleared for this recipient. If it concerns intermediaries, she asks about the intermediaries. Each audience closes with **“Request terms for this project,” “Ask only for an uncovered order”** where feasible, or **“Leave this to domestic procurement.”** Invite either or both. A previously declined contact can leave Marne with the actual domestic schedule and its constraints.

**Tapira · Railway.** Governor Trembolo meets you at the existing junction. A driver leaves his truck running while the manager looks up his collection number.

> **Trembolo:** “The state line would bring two more sheds within reach.”
>
> **Marne:** “One has given us volumes. The other gave us a letter saying they expect a good year.”
>
> **Driver:** “Can I still come through this gate when you finish?”

- **“Assess the engineers' alignment.”** Carry its first freight section, stops and loading access to the route decision after verification.
- **“Compare the state alignment with equal loading access.”** Gate on a credible alternative. Require price, demand, known interests and actual yard terms before approving it.
- **“Hear the towns before fixing the route.”** Hold alignment pending consultation. Return with included and bypassed towns, any obligations to operators or households, and the eligible routes.

**Vila Tamarin · Sanitation.** The engineer reads house numbers through the window. A resident corrects one by pointing to the door across the lane.

> **Engineer:** “The pipe runs past that door. We'd still have to finish the connection.”

If Lara asked about Celia's street, Marne shows it in the survey or gives the confirmed assessment date. Read back an undertaking only if the office received it.

- **“Send the inspected addresses forward.”** Gate on a viable contract route and verified addresses. Fix those addresses for the signature page.
- **“Price a faster schedule.”** Gate on engineering feasibility. Show incremental cost and displaced work on return. Fund only under separate authority and actual funds.
- **“Audit the unfinished work and hold the order.”** Send documented omissions and costs to review. Give residents inspection status and a contact.

On return, **“Use the engineers’ alignment”** or **“Use the priced state alignment with equal yard access”** fixes the eligible route. **“Settle the outstanding access issue first”** keeps procurement open and the award pending. Tamarin’s priced acceleration can be accepted only with authority and available funds, or declined while the verified address list goes forward.

**Tauá Hills · Rare earths.** Iracema Tuyú folds back the map along the survey line. Water from the bank has darkened the bottom of the page.

> **Tuyú:** “A boat picks up schoolchildren here. Your plan sends the road through the landing.”

- **“Return a narrower map to the communities.”** Attach the proposed landing and water safeguards, boundaries and benefits. Await the affected bodies' replies.
- **“Keep independently authorized preparations going.”** Specify area and permitted tasks. Obtain required consent for remaining works and retain any objections.
- **“Hold the site until access is settled.”** Remove tentative dates. Keep desk work available. Bring back an agreed or independently authorized scope with explicit exclusions.

## 3.2 · The customs list and the Strait

*Independent visits. Sources: [Across the Border](../../production/p2-arguedas.md), [Strait](../../production/p2-mondrath.md).*

In St. Veldra, Tomás Arguedas tells Aroun that his son-in-law is from Tekoku. The two of them have learned to leave football out of Sunday lunch. His foreign minister sets a customs list between the coffee cups. One refrigerated truck appears twice under different docket numbers.

> **Arguedas:** “It sat on the border long enough for both offices to open a file. I'd like the two offices to speak to each other.”
>
> **Aroun:** “We could start with a joint list. Anything that changes a rate would go through both governments.”

- **“Draft an equal commission.”** Give both ministries the list and a mandate to draft powers, dispute procedures and approval rules. Arguedas asks to review the text before any announcement.
- **“Ask about a wider partnership.”** Arguedas points to the truck. “Let's see how we do with that first.” Lara can accept the equal remit or request a separate options paper.
- **“Keep the draft to customs and trade.”** The ministers exchange the narrower remit. Include cattle and competing exports if they appear on the docket.
- **“Keep it with the embassies.”** Return the complaints to standing channels.

At the palace, when the Strait notice arrives, Aroun lays it across the shipping map. Vassel's copy carries the same fifteen percent pilotage increase. Aroun gives its effective date. If an audit confirms usable Tekoku port capacity or a functioning Tarev rail alternative with access agreements, she gives the covered freight and remaining exposure. Otherwise Trade uses current shipping capacity.

> **Lara:** “Can we tell the exporters what they'll pay?”
>
> **Aroun:** “Some can pass it through. We'll need the contracts from the others.”

- **“Ask for the calculations.”** Send an acknowledgement and ask about maintenance costs.
- **“Object to the lack of consultation.”** Send a formal protest and brief exporters on the applicable charging schedule.
- **“Invite Mondrath and Vassel to discuss the fees.”** Send the invitation and give a domestic update date if Trade can confirm one.
- **“File the notice for now.”** Trade can relay the notice. No diplomatic response goes out.

*Record dispatch and any later reply separately. The Strait notice and Tekoku's political conditions progress independently. The commission remit authorizes drafting. A treaty needs its own text, approvals and signatures.*

## 3.3 · The production run and the signature page

*Sources: [Made in Kunasai](../../production/p3-strawberry.md), [Pages the bank will sign](../../production/TERM-DIPLOMACY.md#the-pages-the-bank-will-sign), [Signature page](../../production/TERM-GOVERNING.md#the-signature-page).*

At Strawberry, Martin Works sets two phones on the table. A technician takes one straight back to her bench.

> **Works:** “That one still had a test to go through. The other phone's cheaper than ours costs to make.”
>
> **Kaval:** “How much of the cost can your people actually change?”

Works turns his production sheet over to show the advanced components he buys overseas.

- **“Cost temporary protection.”** Choose whether to require measurable investment and productivity conditions. Trade checks consumer cost, authority and compatibility with signed terms.
- **“Develop a university partnership.”** Request verified company contributions and university capacity. **“Price a broader Tekoku hub”** appears where a larger study is feasible. A funded, staffed programme can lend facilities only if Kaval confirms capacity.
- **“Ask Trade to test the subsidy claim.”** Open an inquiry and keep the allegation provisional.
- **“Keep the market open. Bring me your production plan.”** Works agrees to send it.

On the verified company return, Works tells the technician which batch the factory will finish. If the board has postponed investment or cut the local line, he gives the actual date and change. The decision can precede later market-access offers.

At Treasury, Fontes lays out a complete, unexpired offer matching Lara's request, if one exists. He identifies the lender, financed scope, total cost, repayment, procurement and commercial or security rights. Offers covering the same order require revised schedules. **“Take these terms,” “Ask for a narrower schedule or waiver,”** and **“Use domestic funds”** apply to the available offers and authority. Fontes circles the first drawable amount and reads back the continuing obligations. Acceptance goes for signatures and an effective date. Treasury books cash, principal and service on actual release. Missing terms or an unreleased draw can leave a smaller domestic scope or a hold.

Marne turns to the scope page. On rail, he traces the authorized section. On sanitation, he hands over the address list. On the mine, he gives the representative the access map and monitoring terms. The representative is still looking at the first date on the contract.

> **Representative:** “Who do I call if they're late?”
>
> **Marne:** “The office on that page. I'll give you the copy with their name on it.”

- **“Sign the award with independent checks.”** Require an eligible offer, actual funds, verified scope and access, and an authorized review schedule.
- **“Sign the faster eligible offer.”** Show the actual price and earlier dates with mandatory inspections.
- **“Direct the preferred award.”** Require effective decision authority. Disclose the lawful preference or identified departure from process. File the instruction and its basis.
- **“Release the funded work order.”** Show for a lawful existing contract covering the verified scope. Use its own work order and inherited liability references.
- **“Hold the signature.”** Give the representative the status and the next confirmed decision date.

*Complete a new award once all required signatures are present. Record a covered work order separately. Date the first payment when paid and commencement when the crew or operator starts this scope. Commencement starts the project clock.*

## 4.1 · The revised notice

*Source: [Problem on Site and Revised notice](../../production/p3-project-incident.md). Dispatch only for a qualifying incident on active matching work. Otherwise file a routine update.*

Marne brings in the paper the local office put on the board when works stopped. Someone has altered the inspection date in pencil.

**Mine.** Two machines burned below Tauá. The crew stopped without injuries. Salgado gives the crew's report and explains what the inquiry can verify. **“Protect the crew with federal police”** needs an available unit, lawful scope and 10 FC. **“Pause for a joint plan”** goes to Justice, Defense and the district. **“Tell the preferred contractor to make a local arrangement”** requires that contractor. Actual resumption under the arrangement carries the existing toll and corruption consequences. Keep the payment outside lawful public security expenditure.

**Rail.** Two workers died in the bridge crane collapse. The union is asking for the signed log. **“Close the bridge and investigate,” “Pay the families 10 FC and inspect before restart,”** or, with relevant authority and the implicated preferred contractor, **“Remove the contractor from this section and make the allegation public.”** Salgado explains that the inspection record is disputed. The payment requires a receipt and a restart requires inspection clearance.

**Sanitation.** Three houses above Vila Tamarin were damaged in the trench collapse. Residents receive their required notices. **“Seek rebuilding under the contract and inspect the line,” “Release 10 FC for public repair,”** or **“Limit the public statement to the repair schedule.”** The last carries the concealment consequence and leaves the inspection record and residents' rights in place.

**Return when findings or a costed remedy arrive.**

> **Marne:** “They've asked for a date they can leave up for more than a day.”

- **“Resume the cleared, funded scope.”** Copy the exact scope from the inspection order.
- **“Authorize the verified correction.”** Require the priced work, authority and funds. **“Price a smaller safe correction”** appears if the engineer can define it.
- **“Use the supported contract remedy.”** Show the applicable corrective plan, contractual claim or lawful funded replacement. Correct any public allegation the review failed to establish. An independent basis for keeping the contractor off site requires its own record.
- **“Request the missing record and keep the closure.”** Name the document and custodian when available. **“Keep the works closed pending the next report”** is available without a named record. A notice gives the next confirmed inspection date or the office to contact.

## 5.1 · The landing the map left out

*Sources: [Whose roads stay open](../../production/TERM-GOVERNING.md#whose-roads-stay-open), [A place to go, a reason to stay](../../production/TERM-GOVERNING.md#a-place-to-go-a-reason-to-stay), [The coast in season](../../production/TERM-GOVERNING.md#the-coast-in-season), [Meeting at the landing](../../production/TERM-GOVERNING.md#the-meeting-at-the-landing), [Route through the forest](../../production/TERM-GOVERNING.md#the-route-through-the-forest).*

At the Ibira landing a woman waiting for the afternoon boat holds one end of the map while Tuyú holds the other. The recognized representative asks Marne to show her the proposed boundary. Her finger stays on the landing.

> **Representative:** “They spoke to the town hall. We found out when the surveyors came.”

**“Suspend the disputed section”** sends her the map and suspension. **“Offer revised access and safeguards”** proposes boundaries, a protected stop, water checks and a monitoring office. The community takes a copy for its own decision. **“Proceed within established authority”** requires specific authority and preserves the objections and any challenge. A later notice gives the community's response or effective ruling and, independently, which stop the operator can confirm. The woman asks whether she can still catch her boat today. If the operator cannot confirm a stop, the clerk gives her the next time to check.

On the verified forest route, a guide shows you where a group turned back. A ranger asks whether the authorized itinerary can be inspected during the rains. **“Fund a monitored route”** requires cost, partner, consent and operating capacity. **“Review a combined cultural and forest programme”** requires both viable proposals. **“Keep existing access and conservation”** uses current duties. A funded programme returns with an actual visitor ledger and measured conditions. An active inquiry can bring Salgado to discuss independently supported Red Tree territorial obstruction or Blue Piranhas financial activity.

The local service offices provide verified supply routes and the limits of receiving capacity. Planning passes only the land and access terms that those offices can use; household decisions stay with the people concerned.

## 5.3 · The second selection

*Source: [Second selection](../../production/TERM-GOVERNING.md#the-second-selection).*

Fontes has put the unpaid instalments above the free balance. Marne opens the operating report where service has begun or the schedule for outstanding work.

> **Lara:** “Which of these gives people something they can use first?”
>
> **Marne:** “It depends which people. Start with the place each appraisal actually reaches.”

Show only appraisals meeting current authority, readiness and affordability. Open them in any order. Each gives **“Select this first”** or **“Keep comparing.”** If the first round selected a project without an award, offer its revised appraisal under current gates. An existing award gets **“Continue on the signed schedule”** or **“Prioritize a costed extension”** for distinct feasible work. Carry the existing phase and cost once.

| Ibira rare-earth mining | The lawful access path, proposed boundaries and concession price. Quote cleaner extraction only with confirmed technical readiness. |
| Tekoku technology and university hub | The confirmed staffed education and industrial capacity, willing companies and first placement hosts. Use verified Strawberry commitments or a smaller intake supported by other firms. |
| Mognagna water | The costed cisterns, their supply arrangements and the eventual desalination network. Show plant upkeep and settlements outside the first service area. |
| National Railway Integration | The viable alignment, affected towns and operator. The first freight timetable may precede passenger service. |

A selected phase proceeds to independent signature and commencement gates. Ch6 advances on every route.
