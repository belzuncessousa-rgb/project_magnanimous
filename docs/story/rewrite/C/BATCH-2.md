## 6.1 · The second signature

*Sources: [Payment date](../../production/TERM-REVIEWS.md#the-payment-date), [Signature page](../../production/TERM-GOVERNING.md#the-signature-page). Open beside Fontes's final folder for a selected second-round scope that can reach signature. Treat a distinct second priority and any lawful extension as separate decisions. Otherwise use the hold or progress notice.*

Fontes has folded the balance sheet so the payment dates sit beside the construction schedule. The clerk calls in the representative for the first eligible scope. If two projects were selected, the second representative waits with a different contract.

> **Fontes:** “This payment falls before the next budget review. The other dates are in the same folder.”
>
> **Lara:** “And what can we pay today?”
>
> **Fontes:** “The amount on this line. I've counted the draw we actually received and set aside the bills already due.”

Show remaining implementation payments, ongoing costs, outstanding lender obligations and actual receipts. A missed instalment or open review returns through A with the responsible lender and actual cure terms. Recheck the available money after each award or payment. Where a deposit is late, show the amount and eligible smaller domestic scope, if one exists. Existing scopes follow their current signed permits and payment schedule where those remain effective.

Marne opens the signature copy at the scope. The local representative checks the boundary, addresses or facilities against the notice she brought. Use the applicable record.

- **Ibira mine.** Obtain the required consent or independent lawful authority for the named area. Give the works boundary, access, extraction and monitoring terms and the excluded land.
- **Tekoku hub.** Name the laboratory or existing rooms, actual company and university commitments, first intake and funded equipment. Use confirmed places from Strawberry or the other firms.
- **Mognagna water.** Name the funded cisterns, interim supply and any separate plant connection. The municipalities need collection hours and an operator before publication.
- **Rail.** Name the section, junction, access conditions and inspections. An extension has a scope and operator terms of its own.
- **Ibira fluvial network.** Name the eligible landings, permitted access and operator obligations. The representative asks which boats would call and in which season.
- **Housing and sanitation.** Read out the inspected addresses on the eligible bid or covered order. Carry inherited obligations by contract reference.

> **Representative:** “Should I tell people to make arrangements for that Monday?”
>
> **Marne:** “You can give them the works date once the paperwork clears. I'll have the local office send you its copy.”

- **“Sign this scope with independent checks.”** Require eligible award terms, verified local access and authorization, available funds and a dated review that can be added. Record all required signatures before the award takes effect.
- **“Use the earlier eligible schedule.”** Show when there is a verified shorter schedule with priced obligations and all mandatory safeguards. The same signatures and funding gates apply.
- **“Release the covered work order.”** Use where a valid existing contract and effective authority already cover the scope. Record the order, its costs and the scope that goes to site.
- **“Continue the signed phase and hold the extension.”** Available when prior work can continue and the extension remains separately pending.
- **“Hold this signature.”** Give the representative the open condition and next confirmed review, if any. If Lara retained the entire reserve in Ch5, Fontes leaves the project forecast outside the obligations column.

Use actual payment and commencement events to update the ledger and operating clock. A forecasted payment, signed obligation, cash release and opening each have their own date.

## 6.3 · The wording both sides can send

*Sources: [Terms with Arenza](../../production/TERM-GOVERNING.md#terms-with-arenza), [Second offer](../../production/TERM-GOVERNING.md#the-second-offer), [Overlapping conditions](../../production/TERM-DIPLOMACY.md#what-would-have-to-change), [Agreement at the port](../../production/TERM-DIPLOMACY.md#the-agreement-at-the-port), [Strawberry on the market schedule](../../production/TERM-DIPLOMACY.md#strawberry-on-the-market-schedule). Open each eligible encounter separately.*

**Arenza.** In the room with the two translations, Arguedas lays a pencil across the operative clause.

> **Arguedas:** “Our copy says the commission can recommend a change. I think yours says it can approve one.”
>
> **Aroun:** “The customs office had the same question. Shall we read both versions together?”

- **“Keep the commission advisory.”** Match the language on recommendations and the approvals each government retains.
- **“Delegate these technical customs decisions.”** Show only where both governments can delegate the listed matters. Aroun reads the decision list, publication and review clause, and the procedure for an out-of-scope decision.
- **“Keep the agreement to trade and customs.”** Use the authorized narrower mandate. If a cattle concession is actually on the table, read the applicable trade terms and send a public change to the Tapira file once effective.
- **“Prepare an equal-authority alliance text.”** Requires willingness to pursue the wider project and each government's authorization to negotiate it. Record equal standing, member assent and the separate domestic approval tracks. Arenza can refuse a draft that gives Kunasai unilateral control.
- **“Leave the draft open.”** Existing arrangements govern pending discussions.

With a common text, **“Send this version for both approvals”** starts the process. **“Correct the disputed clause”** requires matching text again before submission. Both approved signatures and the effective date determine whether a commission, bilateral trade agreement or foundation instrument is in force. A founding agreement can take effect without any other country acceding. Under an embassy-only route, Aroun still handles ordinary customs problems through established channels.

**Second offer.** Fontes sets the Federation and Zharani folders beside the domestic schedule. Show only offers actually returned. Each identifies the lender, scope, cost and repayment, procurement terms and any strategic or commercial rights. Put already signed commitments beside them.

If the offers overlap, Fontes points to the two schedules.

> **Fontes:** “Both of these clauses cover the same order. If we accept both, the suppliers will each ask for the signed schedule.”

If only one offer is live, he shows which domestic orders it leaves uncovered. If both are compatible on the terms already returned, lay them side by side and permit separate acceptance subject to agreed funding and approvals.

- **“Accept the compatible offer.”** Choose the returned Federation or Zharani text with compatible rights. Give the cost and continuing obligations before confirmation.
- **“Separate the orders and ask both for terms.”** Show when there is a defensible split and independent financing for any uncovered order. Each bank must accept its smaller schedule. If either refuses, return with the remaining actionable offer or the domestic scope.
- **“Ask the existing partner to amend its terms.”** Show the benefits at stake, the exact conflict, and the proposed waiver or replacement clause. The existing terms govern until an amendment takes effect.
- **“Keep the existing agreement and decline the overlap.”** Retain its obligations. Invite proposals for unrelated eligible work only if Lara chooses to.
- **“Use domestic or regional resources.”** Show the independently financed portion and what can continue within actual permits and resources. Keep a smaller or later phase open to further appraisal.

**Port.** With an actionable Strait response or independent offer, meet at the passenger hall. Vassel attends only by agreement. A ferry attendant collects the notices that would have to change. **“Agree the charges and the review date”** uses actual Mondrath consent. **“Use the alternative route where it is ready”** requires usable capacity, territorial standing, transit rights when applicable and a named operator. **“Keep the current arrangements”** leaves the timetable under its existing terms. The attendant changes the notice on the effective date for confirmed calls. Port appointments and wage settlements follow their own records. Strait pressure and Tekoku's status can coexist independently. Use the effective authority at each port.

**Strawberry.** If an actual technology or trade proposal includes a market clause, name the partner, products, rates or access terms and start date. Put the current company plan and the university agreement alongside.

> **Works:** “I need the date before I order parts for the next run. And I'd like to know what our distributors will be allowed to sell there.”
>
> **Kaval:** “The university can teach the course on this agreement. The company still has to confirm which engineers it can release.”

- **“Accept the offered market schedule.”** Send the exact terms for the required approvals and signatures.
- **“Counter with a transition and reciprocal access.”** Give the named phase-in, review date and reciprocal term. The other side can refuse or modify the counter.
- **“Keep the technology terms and remove this clause.”** Seek express agreement to sever it. If the partner refuses, show whether the original offer remains open.
- **“Decline the access offer.”** Follow any linkage in the actual proposal. Current rules govern until a new measure takes effect.

On a written reply, **“Send the matching text,” “Counter their proposed change,” “Accept the original clause,”** or **“Proceed under the agreed severance”** appears according to the actual offer and authority. **“Decline the linked proposal”** closes the new discussion. Verify matching, live terms before sending them for approval. On effect, use actual consumer or commercial figures where measured. Dispatch [Next production run](../../production/p3-strawberry-followup.md) when staffing, output or an actionable domestic proposal is verified. **“Approve the funded university agreement,” “Return the protection terms,”** and **“Issue the authorized trade remedy”** require their own respective unlocked terms and decisions. On the operating return the technician reads confirmed shifts or reassignment contacts. A proposed contribution stays outside the hub's intake until the company confirms it.

## 7.2 · The people who can answer

*Sources: [Before the equipment arrives](../../production/TERM-GOVERNING.md#before-the-equipment-arrives), [A place at the table](../../production/TERM-DIPLOMACY.md#a-place-at-the-table), [Reply from Arenza](../../production/TERM-DIPLOMACY.md#the-reply-from-arenza), [A different delegation](../../production/TERM-DIPLOMACY.md#a-different-delegation).*

**Partner status.** If the alliance is in force and an applicant has agreed to talk, give it a seat and its own obligations. The Veldmoor delegate asks for publication rules and the review of environmental and rights commitments. If a verified concern remains, she points to the decision she needs addressed. A qualified science agreement can proceed separately even if wider accession lacks consent. Vassel gives available sailings and operator commitments.

- **“Put the agreed safeguards in the accession text.”** Send the exact text to existing members and the applicant for their own approvals. Accession starts on its confirmed date. A research placement or sailing still needs an institution or operator to start it.
- **“Offer a narrower sector agreement.”** Use independent authority for a funded science or maritime agreement; a bilateral route can offer it without an alliance.
- **“Keep the members' terms.”** Invite the applicant to answer. A refusal leaves the founding agreement in force.

**Arenzan election.** When an established contact asks about commission work before publication of the campaign schedule, Aroun brings the note. **“Confirm the date privately”** uses the current schedule, with an optional greeting once the relationship supports it. **“Confirm the work publicly”** uses the approved scope. **“Endorse the candidate”** requires an eligible public role and a separate choice. **“Wait for the result”** sends only an acknowledgement. After a certified result and actual transfer, use the incoming delegate's instructions. **“Continue both authorized items,” “Continue the commission only,” “Take up this specific bilateral matter,”** or **“Leave expansion open”** appears as supported. An optional personal call uses actual prior contact and can occur after either election outcome. The two offices carry the signed obligations through the change in government.

**At-risk milestone.** The site clerk asks whether a delivery can move to Friday. Marne checks the operator's paper.

> **Marne:** “That delivery can move. The opening on this sheet depends on a different inspection.”

Show the distinct usable fallback if already costed, including staffing or an operator. **“Open the part that passes”** waits for clearance and confirmed service. **“Fund the documented correction”** needs actual funds and authority. **“Keep the current authorization”** leaves the eligible work running on its confirmed scope. Preserve the dated obligations and unresolved inspection for Ch8. A deferred terminal date or pending review can leave these choices in the ordinary diary while Lara retains authority. If a terminal event actually takes effect, use D's transition and close the term. A threatened Strait closure or negotiations alone leave the calendar live.

## 8.1 · The opening notice

*Sources: [A section in use](../../production/TERM-GOVERNING.md#a-section-in-use), [Copy for the next team](../../production/TERM-GOVERNING.md#the-copy-for-the-next-team). Independent of nomination, ballot status and retirement. Play once for a distinct newly usable phase. Otherwise use the handover schedule.*

The clerk asks which date to put on the notice. The operator gives the first date people actually used the scope. Use a dated household connection, a rail departure, a verified first placement, a supplied cistern, an operating mine phase or the first regular boat stop. **“Confirm the opening for the public record.”** The clerk adds the actual date and exact scope, and notes any further phase still waiting. **“Send the verified scope to the handover team.”** The clerk includes the permit, unpaid instalments and next scheduled review. E can invite the resident, operator or participant to give firsthand evidence. If only preparations are complete, give E the site witness who can describe the remaining gap. Preserve the responsible office and active obligations for the successor.
