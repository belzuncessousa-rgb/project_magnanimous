# A · The first budget

Playable prose and return rows for Ch2.1–2.3. Italic instructions are for integration. Costs, dates and the tally come from the applicable records. A costed district repair may draw on a verified discretionary Education repairs line. The matched pilot leaves that decision pending. The Ibira River Post's reserve vehicle is an illustrative, adjustable Defense line agreed with B.

## Ch2.1 · The working folder

Fontes moves a stack of treasury estimates to make room for your folder. Dara's rota is clipped to the Health sheet. One corner has folded over the afternoon column.

> **Fontes:** “The invoices we could verify are in the obligations column. The inherited services are already in the totals. You can turn a proposal on and off here and see how much of the projected surplus it needs.”

Livia points to a sheet behind the ministry pages.

> **Livia:** “Planning sent the dates for the project you selected. The people preparing the bill asked what they could tell their districts about the work.”

> **Fontes:** “Give them the estimate and the dates Planning used. We'll need a separate decision before we place any order.”

*Show the five inherited totals, verified liabilities and existing debt service once, projected surplus, and draft uses against it. Put GP1's dated estimated exposure on a separate Planning line, distinguishing any payment actually due. In the pilot GP1 has no award or payment and the Fund mission has yielded no proceeds.*

**Ask before marking the folder.** *Optional, once each, any order.*

- **“What would the extra clinic money buy?”** Dara runs a finger down the rota. “Additional outpatient sessions at Sugar Bay General. We can keep the hours patients already use. I need the hospital to confirm who can staff the extra sessions before I give the desk a date.”
- **“What can I count from the Fund?”** Fontes lifts out Royce's paper. “These are his preliminary conditions. Use the receipts on our own schedule for this estimate.” *If Lara requested a longer schedule:* “I've kept the request with his letter. The payments here still follow our existing agreement.”
- **“Where is there room to save?”** Fontes opens the Defense sheet. “Voss identified an overhaul we could defer at the Ibira River Post. Once the inspection expires, they'll have one vehicle in service. If Treasury verifies that the saving covers the gap, we can keep the Health rota and household payments.” *Show the verified saving when available.*

**Mark a working proposal.**

- **“Cost the additional Sugar Bay sessions.”** *Show when scope, setup and ongoing costs are available.* Fontes adds the expansion to the Health draft and shows the share of projected surplus it would use. “This is the request for the expanded ceiling. The appointments already in Dara's book are on the inherited line.”
- **“Start from the inherited services.”** Fontes lifts out the extra sessions sheet. “I'll keep this here in case we come back to it.”

- **“Show me what the overhaul saving could buy at Sugar Bay.”** *Show only if Treasury and Dara identify an affordable, staffable subset at zero net growth.* Fontes lays the two sheets side by side. “The post would lose the reserve vehicle after its inspection expires. Dara could use the saving for the sessions on this page.” Preview the change in both ministries and return to the draft choice. The contraction sheet instead needs this saving to protect inherited services.

Either draft can go to talks. An estimate changes no budget, appointment, project stage or promise.

## Ch2.2 · A figure for the bench

Avros is on the telephone when you enter his office.

> **Avros:** “Ask them which rooms are closed. If the estimate is for the whole building, I need the page for those two.”

He puts the receiver down and straightens a slip from the municipality.

> **Avros:** “The deputy is hearing from parents. Education and the municipality have each sent the application to the other office. He'd like to know which one is supposed to answer him.”

> **Lara:** “Has Education seen the estimate?”

> **Avros:** “I asked the department to send its answer here. The deputy wanted a copy before he went home.”

*If the estimate has arrived, show the dated scope and quote. If it is still pending, Avros says who is preparing it and what they still need.*

*Read D's district term. If an earlier promise about funding, publication or the school would change, Avros reads back what the bench was told. Before circulating revised terms, offer **“I'll explain the change to the bench”**, **“Give them my explanation”**, or **“Keep the earlier terms”** where those terms are feasible. Record the old terms and who heard the change. Seek fresh replies after circulation.*

- **“Send the school through ordinary review.”** *Show when no live conditional undertaking would be displaced, or after Lara addresses that undertaking with the affected deputy.* Avros picks up the slip. “I'll tell him which office has it. Can they give him a date for an answer?” Livia asks Education for its review date. The request keeps its place under ordinary review.
- **“Include the two classrooms in the repair schedule.”** *Show only if the scope is eligible and Education has returned a costed source within its existing discretionary repairs line.* Fontes points to that line. “The department can reserve room here. That would leave less for other repairs under review.” Avros reads the scope aloud. “I'll tell the deputy which rooms you're putting forward.” Confirm the school commitment to the deputy through Avros, on the current publication terms. It is subject to the effective bill and Education's separate decision to proceed. No works order or expenditure is written here.
- **“Keep my undertaking while Education costs the work.”** *Show if Lara gave the conditional commitment and no actionable estimate is available.* Avros folds the slip into his copy. “I’ll tell the deputy we’re waiting on the two rooms. I’ll ask Education when they can send the cost.” Circulate the ceiling without authorizing the repair.
- **“Ask for Education’s answer before deciding.”** Avros sets the request beside the bill. “I’ll let you know which office has the estimate.” Continue to the ceiling talks with the school pending or wait for the estimate if the school is necessary to the version.

**Send a version to the benches.**

- **“Ask Pelan to organize support for this version.”** Avros looks from the ceiling to the Planning schedule. “I'll ask the members who wanted to wait. Give me the dates they can use when they go home.”
- **“Send this version to the members Caio pledged to bring.”** *Show only after the appointment has fulfilled the recorded promise and Mertens has actually undertaken to whip for the bill.* Mertens checks the date on the cover. “I'll call them with this copy in front of me. Give Pelan the same one.”
- **“Go back to the figures.”** Return to the working folder. If a circulated page changes, put the replacement before those affected and ask for fresh answers.

Livia has folded down a corner of the bill to mark the ceiling. In the corridor, the clerk stops a deputy to replace a page in his copy.

> **Livia:** “The clerk sent this version to all the members. Here are the replies we have for it.”

>
> **Livia:** “And the OPP?”
>
> **Mertens:** “They can speak for themselves on the floor. There's no undertaking from their bench.”

*Confirmed promised OPP director and actual whip undertaking.*

Mertens puts his pencil across the OPP column.

> **Mertens:** “Odel has the appointment confirmation. Mantel and I have called the members. These are the ones who agreed to this copy.”

*If replies are still outstanding, he marks the expected replies separately. If the appointee is already confirmed but has yet to take office, use ordinary organizing unless the actual agreement has already been fulfilled under its recorded terms.*

*Ordinary whip route, including an acting or unfilled appointment, or an appointment with no delivered political undertaking.* The organizer who actually contacted the members opens the current version. “These are the replies. I’ve kept the people who are still considering it on the second page.” If no one undertook extra organizing, Livia shows the clerk’s current list and the ceiling the text requests.

*If the school is costed and included, Livia adds:* “Pelan has the school page. I’ll send Education the one the clerk actually files.” *If Lara carried the conditional commitment pending an estimate, she says:* “Education is still waiting for the estimate. I’ve kept it on Avros’s list.”

*Show confirmed supporters and outstanding replies for this exact version. The independent run has a sufficient list organized by Avros without an OPP undertaking. In the transactional run, Mertens's version-bound OPP commitments supply the missing support. Both show the same expanded ceiling. The clerk's actual division settles the vote.*

Fontes opens a second sheet.

> **Fontes:** “This is what we can use if the expansion fails. If we defer the reserve vehicle overhaul at the river post, we can cover the old rota and household payments. Dara's additional sessions would have to wait.”

*Read the verified fallback before offering submission. If the contraction needs its own bill, display that proposal and its vote gate.*

- **“Submit this version.”** Livia hands it to the clerk. A deputy lowers his lunch bag to the floor when the call begins. **Pass:** Livia writes the effective date on the copy she brings you. “I'll send Treasury the ceiling this afternoon.” Avros asks when Education can decide on the works if the repair is included, or who will answer the deputy if it's under ordinary review. **Defeat:** Fontes writes HOLD beside the new sessions. “I'll tell the departments what they can keep paying. The proposed orders can wait.” Use the verified continuing authority or offer the smaller bill.
- **“Circulate the smaller bill.”** *Show if costed and legally available.* Fontes marks the cut and gives the new copy to Livia. The new text goes back to the affected benches, with notice to the deputy if his school commitment is affected. Return to the door when the replies arrive. A negotiated modest ceiling uses the same sequence and its own spendable figures.
- **“Release the slot.”** Livia calls the clerk. **“Wait for revised figures”** leaves the bill pending without new authority. **“Close this attempt”** moves to the verified continuing authority as final fallback.

## Ch2.3 · The signature page

Fontes has brought the payment schedule as well as the ceiling. He moves the papers so you can read both without turning the folder.

> **Fontes:** “This is the authority we can use from the date here. The receipts and payments are on this page. The Fund instalments come out before the ministries get their figures.”

- **“Keep the existing debt schedule.”** Fontes runs his pencil down the instalments. “These dates stand. I'll prepare the signature page from the money Treasury can use.” This is the choice for the three traced runs.
- **“Use the authorized draw.”** *Show only with complete executable terms and authority.* Fontes reads out the conditions, revised service and confirmed receipt schedule. Credit cash and principal on actual receipt. Until then, Lara may wait or sign a schedule affordable from available funds.
- **“Make the additional principal payment.”** *Show for an authorized and affordable repayment with the effect on reserve and service visible.* Debit cash and principal on payment. Bring back the updated schedule.

Fontes gives you the five rows together.

> **Fontes:** “Mark which existing commitments you want to protect. I'll show you what changing the rest leaves for the ministries.”

- **“Build the schedule from inherited services.”** Load the effective baseline. If authority or available funds fall short, name the reductions required before signing.
- **“Move available funds between ministries.”** Choose an eligible reduction or unused line, then an affordable destination or reserve. The affected ministers give Fontes the services and dates for both revisions. Existing obligations remain due.
- **“Show me which proposals the approved ceiling can cover.”** Compare the costed options, their start conditions and the reserve. Set the departmental figures here. Lara gives the programme instructions separately.
