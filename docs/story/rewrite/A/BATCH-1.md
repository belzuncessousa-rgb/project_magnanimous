# A · Taking office and accounting for the first budget

## 1.1 · Inauguration Address

You can hear the crowd from inside the palace. A woman in the front row holds a campaign photograph above her head.

Less than five years ago, you were working as a public prosecutor. People first began to recognise you during the corruption investigation that preceded your election to the Senate. Today, you assume the presidency of Kunasai.

## 1.1 · The Blue Room

Your chief of staff, Livia Harvan, has left a blue folder at each place.

> **Livia:** “I asked everyone to bring an overview. Alexander, could you begin?”

Alexander Fontes worked at the Central Bank. He unfolds a page of receipts.

> **Fontes:** “Growth has been slow. Ibicuí's businesses supply much of our revenue, and farms and trade bring in a considerable share. Most of what we collect keeps existing services running. I still have to verify some of the debt figures.”

Tobias Marne, the Planning minister, keeps a hand on a rolled map.

> **Marne:** “We have towns growing beyond their water mains. Some transport proposals are ready for review. I'm also checking what it will cost to finish works we inherited.”

Livia calls on Owin Dara, a physician who now looks after Health and Social Programs. He takes his glasses from his pocket.

> **Dara:** “I visited a doctor outside Sugar Bay who serves six communities. When she's away, her patients travel to the city. The household payments are also due for renewal.”

Helena Kaval, a former rector from Tekoku, has marked several pages of Education's report.

> **Kaval:** “Some districts are losing pupils before they finish school. Our universities prepare graduates who often find their first job overseas.”

> **Marne:** “If you know an engineer who'd like to stay, send them my way.”

Livia turns to Renata Salgado. You worked with her as a prosecutor. Her ministry now covers federal policing, justice and labour.

> **Salgado:** “Our prosecutors have cases left over from the previous administration. Some involve public officials. We've also had requests to meet the port unions.”

Defense Minister Aldric Voss, a retired general, slides a photograph of a river post out of his folder.

> **Voss:** “At some posts we rely on equipment that's near the end of its service life. I'll have a list ready for the budget.”

Darian Kesht has spent most of his career in Intelligence. He leaves his own folder closed.

> **Kesht:** “Foreign services are active here. We also have reports of criminal groups approaching people in public office.”

> **Salgado:** “If there's evidence a prosecutor can use, my office can take it through the proper channels.”

Lastly Livia calls on Celeste Aroun, a former ambassador who now runs Exterior.

> **Aroun:** “Arennia and Zharan have both asked for meetings. They want to talk about trade and investment. I'd like to make time for Arenza too.”

*Ask once per topic or adjourn.*

- **“What has held back growth?”** Fontes mentions expensive credit and exporters exposed to changes in world prices. “I'd be cautious about relying on a recovery in the first budget.”
- **“How are families receiving their payments?”** Dara says that some local registries can account for each payment, while others rely on intermediaries. He offers to bring the regional figures.
- **“What would you bring me privately?”** Kesht says some reports would identify sources if he went into detail. Livia offers him a separate appointment.
- **“What has kept us apart from Arenza?”** Aroun sets her page down. “We sell many of the same things. Our governments have both been fond of calling themselves the principal power in the region.”
- **“Thank you. We'll take these up separately.”** The ministers gather their folders. Mertens stays in his seat.

Livia opens a map of Ibira. “Red Tree holds territory here. The Blue Piranhas work chiefly through the cities. Renata wants to know what to prepare for your meeting.”

- **“Have Justice and Intelligence review it discreetly.”** Livia requests a joint assessment. Set `cartels_watch_quiet`.
- **“Prepare a public commitment to confronting organised crime.”** Salgado will bring Lara a draft statement and assessment. Set `cartels_watch_loud`, hidden FORCE +1. The draft remains private.
- **“Keep the current approach until the budget.”** Livia keeps existing instructions in place. Set `cartels_ignored`.
- **“Review which enforcement steps can be postponed.”** Livia brings the question to Salgado. Judicial proceedings continue under their own authority. Set `cartels_softened`, hidden FORCE −1.

Mertens retrieves his jacket. “Come by when you're free. I have the figures from Congress.” On exit set `seen_blue_room` and open Counting Votes.
