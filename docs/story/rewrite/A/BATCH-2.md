# A · The final allocation

## 6.1 · The government's free balance

The folder Fontes brings you has the first signed allocations at the back. The new sheet fits on one page. He has clipped a bank confirmation to a line near the bottom.

> **Fontes:** “I closed the figures on the date at the top. Those are the tax receipts we have in the account. The departments sent the instalments they still have to pay. I'd like you to see those before we look at anything new.”

*Refresh the 5.2 estimate as of the displayed close. Show receipts since the previous estimate and their actual dates under D's effective tax terms. Keep scheduled collections in the forecast. Show cleared draws, confirmed payments, outstanding principal, debt service and arrears. Present the original effective ceiling and enforce any tighter line restrictions or continuing authority. Show the five ministry totals, remaining implementation instalments, continuing costs and authorized capital due by date. Include a signed GP2 award only after its own effective signature. The appraisal and its expected first payment sit outside current liabilities. Paid costs are already reflected in cash and authority used. Deduct each remaining obligation once.*

> **Lara:** “Which figure can the ministers use?”

*With free funds:* Fontes moves the clip. “This one. It leaves the reserve in place and covers the payments on the sheet. I'll check each proposal against the line it actually has to use.”

*At zero after commitments and reserve:* “We can cover this schedule. Anything beyond it needs to wait.”

*With a documented gap:* “We can cover the payments through this date. These are the ones still exposed.” He gives you the due dates, amounts and which orders can be held while the responsible office answers.

*Show both cash and authority, and the resulting free balance after authorized commitments and protected reserve, or the actual dated gap. Cash can be positive with no legal room for a proposed use. A shortage on one date stays visible even when a later forecast is stronger.*

### The lender's letter

*Use each relevant dated record once. If no payment or response requires a decision and there is no scheduled Fund review, pass directly to proposals. The domestic pilot with ordinary servicing and no Fund draw often passes without this exchange.*

Fontes takes the notice from the folder. “The bank needs our instruction by the date on the cover. The payment itself is due here.” *Show both dates when the records give both.*

- **“Make the scheduled payment.”** *Show if funded and still due.* Fontes sends the instruction. “I'll attach the bank's confirmation when it comes in.” Change cash, principal to the extent due, and payment status on confirmation.
- **“Ask for dates we can meet.”** *Show if amendment is possible.* Fontes reads back the dates and asks to send the cash schedule with them. Keep the current obligation until an effective agreement replaces it.
- **“Accept the bank's written schedule.”** *Show when an actual offer names the payments, fees and total cost, and authorization and affordability clear.* Fontes shows you the old and proposed dates. “We can send this back signed. I'll change our schedule from the date the bank confirms.” Lara may retain current terms or request a change to a specific offered term. A declined request leaves the old dates; read the refusal and show whatever funded payment or legal response remains.
- **“Pay the overdue amount on the terms stated.”** *Show only after an actual overdue notice, where funds and authority cover the cure and the bank accepts it.* Fontes asks for a receipt and attaches the stated deadline to his copy. Clear arrears only on acknowledgement. If there is no feasible payment, **“Ask for a written cure schedule”** when available or **“Send the notice to counsel.”** The notice's stated clock continues.

*If an effective Fund agreement has reached review, show the condition and the Fund's actual response.* Fontes puts the government's submission beside it. “They've answered the papers we sent. This is the part of the agreement the letter refers to.”

- *Accepted:* “They've given us the next draw date.” Show the dated forecast. Cash changes only once the money clears.
- *Outstanding:* **“Complete the requirement on the agreed terms”** if the office can comply. Fontes asks for the earliest feasible submission. **“Ask for more time”** sends the material available and the proposed date. The existing terms govern until a change takes effect. **“Plan for the draw to be held”** identifies the uncommitted starts that would have relied on it and feasible replacement sources. Existing debt service stays due.

*Where no feasible remedy covers an impending payment, Fontes confirms the residual gap and informs the affected parties. Use the lender's actual written response when it arrives. No lender review expands the original allocation authority. Reconcile the free balance once each effective change clears before taking another decision.*

### The two estimates

*Show either proposal only after Lara requested it and complete terms and funding route have returned. Open both if both qualify. Use separate decisions against the same refreshed ledger.*

*Filing assistance.* A Revenue clerk unfolds the schedule next to the call log.

> **Clerk:** “We've listed the offices that asked for extra hours. The estimate covers the staff and printing. They'll still use the same forms and filing dates.”

- **“Approve assistance at the named offices.”** *Gate on the full scope, lawful line and available funds.* The clerk confirms the scope and sends the schedule to those offices. Record the commitment and its dated costs. Each office enters operation when it confirms its first staffed day.
- **“Cost fewer hours,” “Hold the proposal,”** or **“Decline the proposal.”** The clerk marks Lara's decision. A smaller estimate returns only when complete; a proposal needing a new appropriation can go to the handover file for a later budget. Filing service continues under the existing schedule meanwhile.

*Festival.* The Culture officer turns the proposal so you can see the organiser's signature.

> **Officer:** “This is the request they priced after your visit.”

*Read the selected scope only. For a permanent rehearsal space and music school, show the site, staff, maintenance and dated costs. For the grant, show the listed festival edition and eligible expenses.*

- **“Approve the rehearsal space and music school”** or **“Approve the grant for the listed edition.”** *Show the applicable choice only when its full scope fits its own authority and balance. Show terms before confirmation.* The officer reads back the named site and maintenance plan, or the edition and expenses. Confirm the commitment at signature. An opening or funded festival is entered when the responsible office verifies it occurred.
- **“Ask for a revised estimate,” “Hold the proposal,”** or **“Decline the proposal.”** The officer writes the reason or requested scope onto the cover and sends the response. No new expense enters the ledger.

*On either approval refresh the remaining balance before the other review or final allocation. Separate authority for each use survives the shared affordability check.*

## 6.2 · The signed additions

Livia has put the final sheet next to the original allocations. Dara's office called while Fontes was upstairs. She has left the slip beside the Health row.

*If additional sessions are operating, name the actual desk, hours and date. If funded and preparing to open, give the next verified staffing step. On the contraction route read inherited hours and household support, and show the actual status of the reserve vehicle from B.*

> **Fontes:** “I've kept the money for their existing work on the schedule. You can add to a ministry's instructions using the balance here. The next page shows what each minister can actually take on.”

*Show eligible unchosen initiatives or costed deeper capacity validated by B. Give the ministry, site, scope, setup, upkeep, remaining balance and earliest supported implementation milestone. Terms must fit the surviving authority and the dated cash schedule. Preserve earlier programme scope and effective commitments in all cases. C's awards and A's already approved filing or festival costs are shown as obligations rather than offered again.*

- **“Keep the current allocations.”** Livia closes the proposal pages. “I'll tell the ministries to use the schedule they already have.” Record the retained reserve and any gap. This is the available close when nothing fits, and a voluntary choice even with surplus.
- **“Add [specific eligible scope].”** Fontes places the confirmed source beside the addition. For an uncontested scope, Livia checks that the minister is available for Lara’s call. For a consequential mandate, the responsible minister describes the work and the capacity it requires. *If Health confirms a qualifying addition to outpatient provision:* Dara opens the proposed rota. “These are the sessions we can prepare from this line. The hospital will confirm the first date it can staff them.” *If a lawful, costed institute is eligible:* Voss turns his plan to the page for the host. “They can start preparing intake. Ask me about the research results when we've had time to do the work.”
- **“Sign these additions.”** *Review all selected costs together against the refreshed ledger and same original authority. Offer to remove proposed additions before signing.* Fontes reads the updated totals and remaining reserve. Livia takes the signed instructions to the responsible ministers. Record effective date, scope and funding once per addition. Ministers confirm actual starts and milestones separately. An institute may open within the term. Its twelve-turn return follows elapsed operating time and can fall in the epilogue.
- **“Close without additions.”** Livia puts the draft behind the signed original. Pending proposals can remain in the file with their actual prerequisites. No new instruction takes effect.

## 7.3 · The copy for the next team

Fontes sets the next payment schedule beside the copy he intends to leave with Treasury. Marne arrives with the most recent letter from the local office.

*Play for outstanding dated debt, work or a decision that belongs in the next team's papers. Use C's local report for each project, without replaying a delivery already reported.*

*Selected, unawarded:* Marne lays the appraisal beside the reply. “They asked what they could tell the local office. We sent them a priority. They never received an award.” Show the actual unresolved condition or recorded deferral and any live offer deadline.

*Funded, still unavailable:* Marne opens the amended schedule. “The local office can give people this inspection date. They want to know when they'll have enough to put up an opening notice.” Substitute the actual next payment, consent or other verified milestone if that is what the office has. Show what has been paid, what still falls due and on whose authority.

- **“Put the schedule in the handover.”** Fontes checks the next instalment against the bank confirmation. Marne attaches the authorized work and the separate decisions still open. The file is addressed to the officials who will carry the obligations forward.
- **“Send the local office the confirmed position.”** Marne reads back the decision that can be reported and the next responsible office. Record the notice and its recipients once it is issued. Its delivery leaves any prior service pledge at its actual status.

## Handoffs

- E receives receipts, actual debt and remaining instalments, protected reserve or gap, and dated records of authorized, operating and pending work. Public knowledge follows publication and actual recipients. The schedule is available before candidacy or election is settled.
