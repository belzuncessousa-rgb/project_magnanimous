# Title-theme sketches

Use these as short auditions in Dreamina's audio generation tool. The game is primarily read, so this cue is for the title screen and should establish identity without implying that music will play throughout gameplay. Generate both candidates at the same duration. Ask for instrumental audio only; no voices, lyrics, sound effects, or recognizable existing melodies. Keep the exported generations as unedited source candidates and record the model/date in the inventory.

## A — Institutional restraint

Create an original 30-second instrumental title theme for a thoughtful political narrative game about a newly elected president governing a fictional republic. Intimate, deliberate, humane, and quietly consequential. Sparse felt piano, restrained low strings, and a very subtle modern ambient texture. Let a small, memorable three- or four-note motif emerge, with room for silence and a sense of unresolved responsibility. Contemporary and cinematic, but understated; no patriotic anthem, no military snare, no triumphant fanfare, no vocals, no dialogue, no sound effects, and no imitation of any existing music or artist. Give it a composed, satisfying ending; it does not need to loop.

## B — Civic scale

Create an original 30-second instrumental title theme for a thoughtful political narrative game about a newly elected president governing a fictional republic. Intimate and humane, with a quiet sense of national scale and responsibility. Warm piano and restrained acoustic/orchestral colors, supported by a soft modern pulse; allow the arrangement to widen slightly, but keep the dynamics controlled and the mood morally uncertain rather than victorious. Include a small, memorable three- or four-note motif. Contemporary and cinematic, not a patriotic anthem; no military snare, no triumphant fanfare, no vocals, no dialogue, no sound effects, and no imitation of any existing music or artist. Give it a composed, satisfying ending; it does not need to loop.

## Listening comparison

Compare the two 30-second sketches at matched volume. Listen once without looking at the screen, then alongside the title screen. Note which motif is memorable without demanding attention and whether either sounds too much like a generic political trailer. Do not select based on generation polish alone; a rougher sketch with the better identity is the useful result.
