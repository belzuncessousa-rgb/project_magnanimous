# 7.2 Ending Scenarios

Where the epilogue speech (7. Endings) is the <i>texture</i> of how the term is remembered, the <b>ending scenario</b> is the <i>structural fact</i> of how Lara's presidency ends. Every playthrough resolves into exactly one scenario.

There are two classes: <b>Early Endings</b> (the term is cut short — the epilogue speech never happens) and <b>Regular Endings</b> (the term completes and the speech is delivered before the resolution).

---

## Early Endings

These interrupt the playthrough before P6. They are failure states in the sense that Lara does not finish on her own terms — but they are designed, authored conclusions, not game-over screens.

### Impeachment — "The Corruption Scandal"

Lara is removed from office through the constitutional process. The scandal becomes the defining image of the government.

<b>Trigger logic (provisional):</b> fires when <b>Corruption is too high</b> <i>AND</i> at least one enabling condition is true:

| Enabling condition | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Complete freedom of speech / press | Defined |

<b>Design note — the press freedom irony:</b> the cleaner Lara keeps the press, the more exposed she is to impeachment when corruption is high. A captured or pressured press suppresses the scandal. This is a deliberate tension: protecting democratic norms increases personal political risk. Worth preserving and making legible to the player.

---

### Death — "The Assassination"

A radicalized faction succeeds in killing the president. The factions capable of mounting an attempt: <b>radicalized CV</b>, <b>the Cartels</b>, <b>NF</b>, or <b>the Colonels</b>.

<b>Survival counter.</b> By default the president can survive <b>2 attempts</b>. The third successful attempt against an exhausted counter is fatal. The counter is modified by player choices across the term:

| Choice | Effect on survival counter |
| --- | --- |
| Default | <b>2</b> |
| Defund the Jaguars <i>(elite presidential guard, a holdover from imperial Kunasai — a quick event offers this opportunity)</i> | <b>−1</b> |
| Cut intelligence spending | <b>−1</b> |
| Each investment in Justice & Interior to investigate the corruption of these factions | <b>+1</b> |

<b>How attempts are generated:</b> the number and timing of assassination attempts scales with how radicalized the hostile factions have become (driven by how hard Lara has moved against them, redlines crossed, etc.). <i>Exact generation rules TBD as faction radicalization is modeled.</i>

<b>Design note — the Jaguars:</b> new lore element. An imperial-era elite guard surviving into the republic. Defunding them is ideologically satisfying (dismantling a monarchic relic) but materially dangerous (it lowers the president's protection). This is a clean values-vs-survival choice. → Add the Jaguars to the World/history section.

## Regular Endings

The term completes, the epilogue speech is delivered, and the playthrough resolves into one of three structural outcomes. These are <i>containers</i> — the sphere outcomes from 7. Endings fill in what kind of reelection / defeat / retirement it was.

| Ending | One-line frame |
| --- | --- |
| <b>Reelection</b> | The mandate is renewed. Lara governs a second term, carrying every accumulated cost and credit forward. |
| <b>Not Reelected</b> | The voters chose someone else. The speech was a closing argument that did not land. Lara leaves office with her record intact but her project unfinished. |
| <b>Voluntary Retirement</b> | Lara chooses not to run. Whether this reads as dignified exit, exhaustion, or quiet defeat depends on the sphere outcomes and her personal-legacy state. |
