## Lore

### Identity & Character

Tapira was a late colonised state. It saw some mineral exploitation in the early colonization, but its real economic boom was during the first republic, with the few landlord families from that time still enduring to this day. Sparsely populated, with infrastructure mostly by roads, this place ssem to be frozen 50 years in the past, the only real change was the arrival of the Tabernacles, often patronized by the rich landlords as a means to entertain the locals. Since those often have direct influence on what people think, they basically control the electorate in the region.

### Economic Reality

<p>
Though fully dependent in grains and cattle, this is the principal economic activity of  Kunasai, making it a global reference in the production of meat and grains. The landlords based here also control most of the agrobusiness in Ibicuí, Mognagna, and Ibira and have many politicians in their pockets. 
</p>

### Political Landscape

Of course, CP and FFF dominate the political landscape of  Tapira, with room for little else. Modern ideas of socialism or progressivism have not reached Tapira, thus CV, PM and CA have no reach there. Possible NF electorate is often consumed by FFF.

### Historical Wounds

Since it is most under control of oligarchs, Tapira feels little for what the rest of the country is going through.

### Relationship with the Capital

The agrobusiness depends somewhat in protectionism and favorable policies (and eventual bailouts) from the central government and they historically have OPP and CP politicians in their pocket to do their bidding. This status quo works well to everyone so far.

### Arcs involving Tapira

Ibira may look as the most steady flow of cash and being a global power in food production may give some levarage in international negotiations. The dependency on the agro goes both ways though, as their operations are not easily moved. Plantations though, have their seasonal risks…
