## Lore

### Identity & Character

Ibicuí is all the hot mess of a region in frank, rapid urbanization. Two megacities formed in the last 50 years: Katanaí and St. Veldra. Both old settlements, dating from the 1700, gained importance as Ibicuí saw the development of tobacco farming. The position close enough to most regions, and the prosperity of the tobacco cultivation in the region coincided with the industrial revolution, though it arrived relatively late in Kunasai. The rapid industrialization drained people from all around the world and from neighboring states Tapira and even more so Mognagna. Later on the highly dense cities became home to most offices and international corporations, attracting also people from Tekoku seeking the plurality of opportunities. The diversity and cosmopolitan states of those two cities and a handful of other industrialized poles also fomented the  development of a modest railway system that further facilitated the success of the region. The port of  Katanaí took place as the prime importing and exporting door from Atiaiaba in Tekoku and has overshadowed it since.

The dynamic urban space, though, did not completely replace the rural elite, especially in the eastern part of the region. There, landlords often from Tapira, still hold much power and influence.

The diversity from this region represents well how the economic and population heart of  Kunasai represents its county, being the main stage for all political parties and clashing ideologies.

### Economic Reality

Mostly focused in idustry in the countryside's big cities; tobacco gave way to wheat and cotton on the countryside and financial and services on the largest of cities, the state have oportunities on all spheres of the economy.

### Political Landscape

Parties hardily dominate Ibicuí, as its diversity may well induce. Most popular parties are the PM and CP, but radical CV and FFF have earned their places amongst operatives and periphery workers. CA has a place among the workers in the financial district and consultancy agencies. Public opinion often comes and goes from NF as more leftist governments get involved in scandals.

### Relationship with the Capital

Ibicuí often feels as the real capital of Kunasai is St. Veldra, as it is the single powerhouse of the country, comprising alongside its satellites of 10% of the population and 15% of the nations GDP. Yet, there is no resentment agains the capital itself and the state has its trongest presence here.
