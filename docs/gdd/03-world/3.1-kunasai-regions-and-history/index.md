## Country at a glance

Fictional country in the northern hemisphere of Arvane. Inspired by contemporary Brazil: political crisis, colonial exploitation legacy, deep inequality, sharp regional contrasts.

- <b>Population:</b> 280 million
- <b>Capital:</b> New Veldrania — planned city at the convergence of Tapira, Mognanga, and Ibicuí
- <b>Continent:</b> Arvane (Equivalent to South America, but located in the northern hemisphere)

## History

### Colonial period (\~300 years before game)

A single Veldran power — <b>Veldra</b> — colonizes Kunasai entering from the north through Tekoku. Colonization is extractivist: mineral extraction in Tapira, agriculture and timber in Ibicuí, Tekoku as export port. Ibira is largely bypassed due to dense forest — indigenous peoples survive there in greater numbers. Enslaved people trafficked from Ondara create the ethnic mixture that defines Kunasai today.

### Independence (\~150 years before game)

Veldra suffers a severe internal crisis and loses the capacity to maintain its empire. The Kunasaian colonial elite — Veldran by descent, Kunasaian by identity — negotiates separation without popular revolution. Independence is an elite pact: the country is born free on paper, but colonial power structures remain intact. Indigenous peoples and descendants of Ondara are excluded from the founding compact.

### Oligarchic Republic (\~150–80 years before game)

Kunasai operates as a nominal republic dominated by landowning families from Ibicuí and merchants from Tekoku. Constitutions, elections, parliaments — all theater. The Arennian Federation grows to the south and begins treating Kunasai as a market and zone of influence.

### Military Dictatorship (\~80–25 years before game)

Economic crisis and fear of labor movements — amplified by Zharan's global influence — provide pretext for a military coup. The dictatorship lasts decades: developmentalist early on (roads, industry, infrastructure investment in Mognanga), increasingly repressive later. Political persecution, disappearances, censorship. The Arennian Federation tolerates and occasionally supports the regime. The dictatorship never reaches Ibira meaningfully — the forest absorbs it.

### Redemocratization (\~25 years before game)

Internal exhaustion — unions, student movements, progressive church — and shifting international winds force a negotiated transition. The military exits without being tried as part of the compromise in the famous Amnesty Act. A new constitution is promulgated with great promise.

### Current crisis (\~16 years before game to present)

After the two presidents following redemocratization, a cycle of political exhaustion begins: corruption exposed across all parties → population swings to extremes → extremists govern poorly or also corrupt → population swings back. The player assumes the presidency in the middle of this cycle — elected as a reaction to the previous extreme, already governing with depleted institutional trust.
