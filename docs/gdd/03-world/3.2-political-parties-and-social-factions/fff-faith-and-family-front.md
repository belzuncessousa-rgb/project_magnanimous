### Relationship with the Tabernacles

<i>The Tabernacles are FFF's organizational backbone — and the Coronéis' instrument in Mognagna and Tapira. This is the hidden link between religious legislation and land reform.</i>

### Current strategy

### Internal divisions

Pastor Baal Oportunists - Baal lead the faction that pursues personal glory and riches at the expense of the believers. They are the most interested in getting involved in politics out of the scope of faith

Pastor Muul Humanists - Muul lead the faction that actually believes in their own sermons and is more open to negotiating with the Communion. Their political objective is to actively improve the CoL of the believers and to reestabilish faith as a moral guiding hand to politics and state religion

### Arcs involving FFF

- The religious arc may play an important role or be mostly ignored throughout the play, but either way it will affect FFF opinion and influence. It will be divided into two smaller constitutional amendments and a larger discussion:
  - Medium: Taxing to private churches and tagging donations as profit
  - Small: Concessions to the Communion at the request of the Holy Father, who wants to reinvest in Kunasai and revert loss of influence - this can be further steered towards accepting the Holy See visit, deny it, or make it a big event
  - Arc: Increased state atheism or religiousness (Up to 2 steps in each direction)
- Low education increases the effects of Tabernacle propaganda (both if they are pleased or displeased with government). High education decreases it.
