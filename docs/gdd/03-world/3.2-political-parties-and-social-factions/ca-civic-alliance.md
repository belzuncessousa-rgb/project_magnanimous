## Lore

### History & origin

CA is the newest political party; the last election was the very first presidential election in which they participated. Their progress and popularity may be intrinsically connected to the confidence people have in the democratic system, and to the education level in the country. This is the party with the most chance to win or lose public support during this term.

### Ideology in practice

<i>A very ideological party, they will fight for their agenda, opposed to anti-education and pro-religion, and bargain investments for the rest.</i>

### Current strategy

Though new, they will not negotiate on ideals and will not accept anything quiet. Their paramount objective is to see the election via literacy reform go though durting this term.

### Internal divisions

Very concise and still relatively small, this party has no internal schism, rather risking absorb meritocratic section of CP, NF, PM, and even OPP.
