## Lore

### History & origin

CP, along with PM and OPP, is amongst the most established and older political parties in Kunasai. With its creation dating back to the stabilization of the first republic, it was banished a couple of times during its history. It is home to all the capitalist lovers who fully embrace the beauty of a free market and the symphony of supply and demand rules. Essentially capitalist, CP has the best history with OPP, as the fundamental axis of money and political power. CP has managed to avoid corruption scandals that hit PM and OPP, even though it was deeply involved in the schemes, because most of the media is either bought or owned by CP members.

### Ideology in practice

Any investment that focuses on increasing CoL and has no impact on GDP will cause relations with CP to deteriorate. Regardless, increase in GDP will be determinant in long-term relations with CP, as long as there is no overgrown state. They back or oppose minor or unrelated constitution based on past decisions, with only important constitutional provisions open for negotiation.

### Current strategy

CP has no particular beef with PM as it has been in the political game long enough. They mainly look to increase business and maintain the money in the hands of the rich.

### Internal divisions

Industrialists and capitalists with Lucas - A latent despise for the agroindustry, the industrialist long for a true minimal state, with the sole responsibility

Ruralists with Vando - Deeply oligarchic and religious, the ruralists are all in for free market and small states when it comes to taxes and alfandegary tributes. Ruralists understand they are still the economic heart of Kunasai and demand that their land rights are always protected, as well as eventual bailouts during bad harvests. That is, they should not pay the state, but the state must protect them.

### Arcs involving CP

- Agroindustry may suffer from increased regional relations (regional bloc path) and from too free market, which may cause them to secede from CP and, of course, antagonise the current government.
