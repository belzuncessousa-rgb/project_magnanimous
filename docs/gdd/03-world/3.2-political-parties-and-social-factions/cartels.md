## Lore

### Identity & history

<i>Native to Kunasai — product of inequality and state abandonment, not foreign import. The cartles are well-established and either grow or fabricate their products inside the jungle and more openly in neighboring Tarev. The Cartels are not particularly subject to bribery as they assert their dominance with violance. Since it is a lucrative deal, they are well-equipped and battle-hardened.</i>

### Current concerns

<i>Active plots throughout: territorial control, rare earth extraction, international pressure on Kunasai to act (which means ceding sovereignty).</i>

### Internal divisions

Obviously, the largest cartels have an uneasy truce, which the player can exploit.

### Arcs Involving the Cartels

The cartel handling has one main arc, but it is mentioned in several arcs/decisions. Regardless of investment, the player will have to handle them (or actively decide not to), but antagonizing them without reducing CtA will have adverse consequences. The other arcs involving the Cartels are investment (in J&I and in Military) and military cooperation (also two ways, cooperating with the Federation to crack them down or with Tarev to cooperate with the cartels).

1. Handling (main Arc)
   - At the end of the first meeting with the VP and CoS, CoS will brief the president on the Cartels and inquire whether they should take action.
     - Start monitoring them more closely: increase suspicion, decrease capacity to act
       - Loudly: increase power, Federation opinion …
     - Ignore the problem
       - Ignoring or monitoring allows to further pressure for control in Ibira, go back and loosen up or keep current pressure
         - Not going back allows for an effective purge, which result will vary depending on CtA vs Nations Military Prowess
     - Soften up and compromise security to not provoke them:  opposite of 1st + decrease CoL in Ibira…
       - Conciliation branches to one scene that can freeze relations at their state, retract their decision or further conciliate
         - further conciliation branches to the opportunity to cooperate with Blue Piranhas to topple Trees
