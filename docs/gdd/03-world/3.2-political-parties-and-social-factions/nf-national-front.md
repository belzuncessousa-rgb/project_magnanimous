## Lore

### History & origin

The National Front was created by Henrick Daur for the second election after redemocratization as a reflex of an increasingly international submissive Kunasai at the hands of Tercilio Luz (the PM president elect) and the economic stagnation that followed, accusing the ruling parties of "tepid" politics. There has been no president from NF yet, but their presence has grown during the period of economic stagnation.

### Ideology in practice

A strong sense of nationalism (IDEN), presence of the state on daily life of people (WELF) and a strong, imposing military are paramount for this party, but their approach to faith (and FFF) and economic systems (libertarian if needs to be aligned with CP and CA or interventionism to align more with VC) may yet be defined to ensure their success.

### Current strategy

NF is actively trying to sabotage PM government, as a strong right swing in Ibicuí is the way to win the next elections, when they can actually implement their policies. Cooperating with this party as Lara will be the hardest.

### Internal divisions

Solano "blame the outside" - Solano and his followers believe in territorial integrity and population integrity, focusing on the Kunasaian identitarianism and blaming the lack of development in the foreign nations exploiting Kunasai.

Henrick "internal blame" - The southern provinces and the Ondaran and indigenous populace drag the advancement of the country; they should be either cut out or recolonized by the civilized northern Veldrans.

### Arcs involving NF

- If the proportion of investments in Tekoku and Ibicuí are less than their contribution to national GDP, NF will get proportionally more popular on those states (calculations done separately)
- If (a path of submission/aliance to the Federation or Zharan were followed OR Mondrath was enraged) AND (the proportion of investments in Tekoku is ≤ 1,5x its contribution to national GDP OR CoL ≤ 65%), the separatist movement will trigger.
