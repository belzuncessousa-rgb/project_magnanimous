## Lore

### History & origin

PM is one of the oldest political parties. Its roots are closer to what today is CV, the Workers United Front was formed not much before the military coup. Banned soon before the coup, a water down version of it was the first to elect a president after redemocratization and has alternated in power with CP and ocasionally OPP since then. This election, MP was lucky that CP previous government was very unpopular and shifted popular opinion towards the left. Its coalition for presidents with CV and OPP was, of course, also paramount.

For Lara players: for Lara, assuming the presidency means also that she may have to think her own way and choose to put hers, the country's or the party's interest in first place.

### Ideology in practice

PM will follow Lara through most of her decisions, until second in command suspicion is above 50. At which point, they will stall Lara's policies unless directly bribed (increase corruption).
