## Lore

### History & origin

<i>The party that survived every regime by being for sale to every regime. Has the stablest electoral base, large enough to be the main tie-breaker. Their apoliticalness makes them basically immobile, both to increase or decrease their support.</i>

### Ideology in practice

<i>The most reliable legislative partner and the most politically embarrassing one to admit needing. They will always have a price. All that political capital, though, always comes with some sort of corruption that can be exploited by enemies later on. Also, the party has no “memory”; the price of each support is independent of historical relations. </i>

### Current strategy

Keep the status quo, while feeding the rich political chaste of the party.
