## Lore

### History & origin

VC predated redemocratization and was established by Alexander Zhukorovich, a Refugee from the communist Skatrana Peoples Republic, who received Kunasaian nationality and at first affiliated with PM. He grew disgruntled with their tepid policies towards capitalism, VC spun off, was banned much before the military coup, and came back a few years still after its end. Ziara studied at the Sugar Bay Federal University with Professor Amber Babau, internationally recognized by his book: A Tale of Humankind, reveered by its unique views on society's problems and insightful views in comunist rule, with people going as far as saying he created a new school of thought: the Kunasaian Communism.

### Ideology in practice

CV has always been amassing power to a Peoples Coup if needed be. Working in a subtle way as not to raise too much suspicion. An armed coup though, is only intended as a last resource, as though by Professor Babau, since stability through the communist government implementation is often better for the people than speed, and if they can see tangible and meaningful ways to achieve their goals through the current system, they will. That said, results are more important than populism, as they don't really need public approval to apply the People's Dictatorship.
