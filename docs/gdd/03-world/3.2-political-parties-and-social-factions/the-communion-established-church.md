## Lore

### Identity & history

The Communionist church was present since the beginning of Kunasai colonisation. Both for good and for evil, it has done much for the history of the country. The missions were the first to brave the jungles of Ibira, and most ok Tekoku early settlements of Tekoku were evangelizing missions.  Sugar Bay Cathedral is the oldest Cathedral in the northern hemisphere and the second-oldest archbishopric. Despite its turbulent times during the late Middle Ages, it is undeniable that Communionism is entrenched in Kunaisaian society, as it can be observed in its national holidays and city names.

Recent administrations by Holy Fathers saw an increased humanism on the side of thye Commuionist church, preaching and acting in practice what was always the rhetoric: love your neighbor and be good. Ironically, that may have come too late, as it has increasingly lost faithful to more fervent branches such as the Tabernacles.

### Current concerns

<i>The slow displacement by the Tabernacles — historically dominant institution losing ground to a faster-growing rival Tabernacle churches and increased atheism. Not only the reduce influence, but also the genuine concern of loosing people to market religion.</i>

### Internal divisions

Now is not the time for division, and most high ranking priests know that, nevertheless, entrenched corruption and scandals of libertinage still haunt the Communionist church.
