## World at a glance

Kunasai is a fictional country in the northern hemisphere of Arvane (analog: Brazil). Population \~280 million. Young republic with a long memory — colonial exploitation, a military dictatorship that ended 25 years ago, and a current political crisis driven by corruption cycles and institutional exhaustion.

The capital is New Veldrania— a planned city at the convergence of Tapira, Mognanga, and Ibicuí.

<b>The geopolitical trap:</b> The Arennian Federation (analog: USA) and Zharan (analog: China) compete for influence over Kunasai. Every move toward one repels the other. The player can exploit the competition — but only while remaining genuinely uncommitted. Compass accumulation makes this harder over time.
