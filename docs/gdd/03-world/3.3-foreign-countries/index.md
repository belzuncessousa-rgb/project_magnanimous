The world is currently divided into three basic choices: To enter into political submission with the Arennian Federation; to increase it’s economical and technological dependence with Zharan, or to invest in multilateralism in international relations, increasing diplomacy and trade between medium and small nations. There is, of course, the option of isolationism and Autarky.

## Strategic notes

- <b>The Strait:</b> Vassel and Mondrath together form a narrow strait on Kunasai's western coast. Any diplomatic crisis with Mondrath is a national economic emergency.
- <b>The Eastern Front:</b> Tarev, Orvane, and Selvara form a Zharan-aligned belt along Kunasai's entire eastern border. Combined with Cabral to the southwest, Kunasai is effectively encircled on three sides by Zharan influence.
- <b>The Arenza opportunity:</b> Highest-return foreign relationship. Slowest to build. A confirmed Kunasai-Arenza bloc reorganizes continental trade and forces both superpowers to recalculate.
