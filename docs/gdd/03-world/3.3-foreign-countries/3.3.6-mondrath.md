## Lore

### Identity & Character

<p>
Mondrath share many of its colonial characteristics with Tekoku. Close to the first colonization and a relatively bland geography, the few non-exploration settlements took place there. Later, but still under Veldran rule, the extraction of oysters and pearls from the waters close to the Mondrath Strait breathed a new economic boom in the region. The Duchy of Mondrath was created as a branch from the royal family itself long before the colonisation and that portion of land was entirely under the Duke’s control, with the duke answering to the Viceroyalty of Kunasai. 
</p>

<i>With the collapse of the metropolis due to widespread war in Vedrath, Kunasaian local elite seized the oportuninity and deposed the Viceroy and installed a republic. The Duke, as the sole defendant of the perpetuity of the monarchic system, decided to not join Kunasai in their independence and instead and declare the Archduchy of Mondrath as independent nation</i>

### The Strait

With the strait being primal on their own econoy, and sharing the strait with Vassel, Mondrath does not use the strait at all as a prime bargain. They do notice their power to hurt Kunasaian trade, but using it would also bring unwanted anger from  the trade-lovers great powers.

### The Veldran Question

Should not be due to the frontier guard and flags changing, one could go from Tekoku to Mondrath without really noticing that they were in a different country at all. The entrenched veldran identity present in both nations could make one think why mondrath is not part of Kunasai or, from another point of view, why Tekoku is not part of Mondrath.

Though relatively isolated, Mondrath is quite well-developed and has a good CoL that could cause disgrutled Tekokuans to push for a union with Mondrath.

<p>
On the other hand, a fervent nationalist Kunasai could argue that since the Viceroyalty, Mondrath was an integral part of the country and theirs by right. 
</p>

Another way, the assyncron authocratic rule of the Duke could be carfully used to convince Kunasains, and the world, that Mondrath is home to essentially Kunasaians that are oppressed by the monarchy and that they have the duty to go save and liberate them.

In a second term, an autocratic Kunasai that could acomodate the Duke’s form of government, could diplomatically reunite with Mondrath as not only culturally, but also politically, are so close to each other.

### Relationship with Kunasai

At the beggining of the game, Mondrath mostly want to be left alone, it can easily increase to actively bad relations with the duke if any form of threat is posed to them and at best can transform into a modest trade. (for the first act)
