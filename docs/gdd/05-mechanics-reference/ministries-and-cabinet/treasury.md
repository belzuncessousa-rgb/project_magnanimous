## Standing Themes <i>(fire regardless of budget)</i>

<i>The ministry's baseline reality — always active. Interacts directly with the debt meter.</i>

<ul>
<li>
Both during 1st and 2nd budget: inheriting a serious debt from previous governments, the interest mounts around 11% of initial GDP. At chapter Budget1, the player may choose to start paying the principal, borrow more, or maintain status quo. At Budget2 the player may again choose to borrow more (with conditions based on current relations and debt/GDP ratio), maintain status quo, or now start to pay the principal.
</li>
<li>
Tax Reform. At the beginning of chapter 3, a tax change will be discussed, and if any change is chosen to be implemented will have to be run through Congress (?). Tax changes affect the tax mechanics.
</li>
<li>
Create national/privatize companies? 
</li>
</ul>
