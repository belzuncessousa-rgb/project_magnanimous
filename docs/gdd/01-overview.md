# 1. Overview

## Logline

You are Lara Vento — senator from Ibicuí, fifteen years of organizing, legal aid offices in the periphery, slow building. Last night you were elected President of the Republic of Kunasai. Now you govern.

---

## Genre & Tone

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| <b>Genre</b> | Political narrative RPG |
| <b>Style reference</b> | Suzerain (Torpor Games) |
| <b>Engine</b> | Ren'Py (Python-based VN engine) |
| <b>Platform target</b> | Steam (PC/Mac) |
| <b>Language</b> | English |
| <b>Art style</b> | Hand-drawn illustration — painted backgrounds + character portraits |
| <b>Audio</b> | Licensed ambient music (no voice acting) |

<b>Tone:</b> Grounded, melancholy, occasionally funny. Kunasai is not a cartoon — it has colonial scars, inequality, genuine hope, and genuine rot. The game never preaches. It shows consequences.

---

## Scope — v1

- <b>Playable archetype:</b> SOC — Lara Vento (Movimento Popular)
- <b>NEO</b> (Edvar Solano, FN) and <b>CAP</b> (Lucas Belzens, PC) are reserved for DLC
- <b>6 periods</b> (\~42 scenes total)
- <b>One four-year presidential term</b> + optional second term in Period 5–6
- The two unchosen archetypes appear as rival NPCs throughout

---

## What makes it different

- <b>No right answer.</b> Every coalition costs something. Every policy has a compass footprint.
- <b>The compass is invisible.</b> Players feel the consequences of their choices through narrative, not a dashboard.
- <b>Characters remember.</b> Loyalty, suspicion, and hidden agendas accumulate across all six periods.
- <b>Endings are earned, not chosen.</b> The ending the player gets is determined by the sum of all decisions — there is no "pick your ending" menu.
