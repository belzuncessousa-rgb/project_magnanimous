# 4.2 Vice-Presidents

One of three is chosen at game start. The choice signals coalition and has hidden costs. Each VP has explicit mechanical bonuses and a conditional trigger.

---

## Caio Mertens — O Ponte

<b>Party:</b> POP · <b>Region:</b> Ibicuí (capital) · <b>Archetype:</b> Technocrat

Compass: Econ +5 · Welf 0 · Ident +2 · Force −1 · Struct +4 · Power +5 · Relig +3

Loyalty (SOC): +1 · Suspicion: 10 · Capacity to Act: 65

<b>Passive bonus:</b> +1 IPI per period

<b>Conditional trigger:</b> If compass Power \< +2, accumulates Suspicion +5/period

<b>Wants:</b> Maintenance of the status quo and of POP's power and influence dynamic.

<b>Fears:</b> Being used as a facade while real power operates without him; authoritarian drift that exposes the party.

<b>Hidden agenda:</b> Undeclared contact with Arennian Federation financiers. Not an agent — but the relationship exists.

<b>Arc:</b> Functional VP → silent dissident / transitional figure in crisis.

<b>Endings:</b> Never plots. Can assume government in Voluntary Exit or Legitimacy Death.

---

## Marco Prestes — O Leal Ideológico

<b>Party:</b> VC (when SOC is played) · <b>Region:</b> Ibicuí (periphery) · <b>Archetype:</b> Ideologue (fixed)

Compass (SOC variant): Econ −7 · Welf +8 · Power +6

<b>Background (SOC variant):</b> Community organizer from Ibicuí periphery, arrested three times on charges that were each time embarrassing.

Loyalty (SOC): +8 · Suspicion: 5 · Capacity to Act: 50

<b>Passive bonuses:</b> Allied party loyalty +2 permanent. Political instability impact −30%. +1 PT per period.

<b>Conditional trigger:</b> If compass diverges ≥3 points on Marco's heaviest axis → public resignation. Effect: Stability −15, Approval −12, Allied party loyalty −4 permanent.

<b>Wants:</b> That the government fulfills its promises — does not negotiate the central agenda.

<b>Fears:</b> Being complicit in a government that betrayed its voters.

<b>Hidden agenda:</b> None. He is genuinely what he appears to be — which makes him the most dangerous character for opportunistic players.

<b>Arc:</b> Loyal believer → public resignation / historical figure of the mandate.

<b>Endings:</b> If resigned, can legitimize moral opposition. In Voluntary Exit with completed agenda, natural name for continuity.

---

## Amanda Troes — A Parceira

<b>Party:</b> No affiliation · <b>Region:</b> Ibicuí (capital) · <b>Archetype:</b> Opportunist (absolute loyalty to a person, not self-interest)

Compass: Econ 0 · Welf +2 · Ident +1 · Force −2 · Struct +1 · Power +4 · Relig +2

Loyalty (SOC): +9 · Suspicion: 0 · Capacity to Act: 45

<b>Passive bonus:</b> All ministers' Suspicion −10 at start. +1 PT per period.

<b>Conditional trigger:</b> In coup attempt or assassination — early detection chance +40%. If president dies anyway, Amanda can force a genuine investigation without being contained.

<b>Wants:</b> That the president reaches the end of the full term alive and healthy.

<b>Fears:</b> That politics corrupts the president in ways she doesn't detect in time.

<b>Hidden agenda:</b> None political. She keeps records of everything — as protection, not as a weapon.

<b>Arc:</b> Invisible shield → only reliable witness / unexpected public voice.

<b>Endings:</b> Never plots. Early detector in coup endings. Organizes transition behind the scenes in Voluntary Exit. Exits with reputation intact in Legitimacy Death.

<b>Note:</b> College friend of the president. No ideology — has shared history. Unbribable because she wants nothing beyond what she already has.
