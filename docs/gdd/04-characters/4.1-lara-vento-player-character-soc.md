# 4.1 Lara Vento — Player Character (SOC)

## Character sheet

<b>Role:</b> President of the Republic of Kunasai (playable archetype)

<b>Party:</b> Movimento Popular (MP)

<b>Region:</b> Ibicuí

---

## Background

Senator from Ibicuí. Fifteen years of organizing — legal aid offices in the periphery, slow building, community work before politics. Elected President as a reaction to the previous administration's failures. She believes in what she says, which is her greatest strength and her most exploitable vulnerability.
