## Interface system
- Use deep emerald for persistent control surfaces, warm ivory for reading and decision surfaces, and restrained brass for selected, required or primary states. Keep danger red reserved for crises and harmful outcomes. Use Source Serif 4 for titles and Inter for compact controls and data.
- Keep the ledger/HUD, Menu, Actions and End Turn available on the player screen. Do not put the ledger on the strategy table, and do not show an Agenda. The tablet contains Home, News, Social, Codex and Reports; News and Social use current published material, Codex lists people met, and Reports collects official correspondence. At compact widths, retain every persistent control and resource; rearrange them into deliberate rows and let long panels scroll.
- Region and foreign-area map targets are keyboard-operable. Focus, hover, selection, targeting validity and unavailable state must remain visually distinct. Scene pins keep their type glyph, brass ring for required scenes, and red only for crisis status.
- Menus and modal choices announce themselves to assistive technology, move focus into the open panel, keep keyboard focus inside, close with Escape, and return focus when dismissed. Toggle choices expose their selected state.
- The presidential tablet is a digital instrument with a tactile case, clear app rail and restrained brass accents. A visible tablet control remains available from the map; the device can be opened from the international view without zooming all the way out. News preserves newspaper mastheads and outlet selection. Social uses concise cards attributed to existing publication names from the current edition; opening a card returns to that outlet’s full article. Do not add fictional account holders, user posts, replies, reactions or engagement numbers until their content and mechanics are approved. Reports opens an existing official report and marks it read. Home gives all four apps a clear entry point. Plan an introductory tutorial for discovering the tablet later.
