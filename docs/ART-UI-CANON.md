## Interface system
- Use deep emerald for persistent control surfaces, warm ivory for reading and decision surfaces, and restrained brass for selected, required or primary states. Keep danger red reserved for crises and harmful outcomes. Use Source Serif 4 for titles and Inter for compact controls and data.
- Keep a fixed paper turn report, Menu, Actions and End Turn available on the player screen; the report carries national arms, chapter/date and current resources. Give End Turn a distinctive tactile control; put outstanding required matters in its tooltip instead of a separate message, and do not show an Agenda. Keep game menus clear and organized with labeled sections and compact icon-led rows. At compact widths, retain every persistent control and resource; rearrange them into deliberate rows and let long panels scroll.
- Region and foreign-area map targets are keyboard-operable. Never add generic rectangular focus or selection outlines. Region feedback follows the actual SVG boundary; other controls use shape, color or surface changes without an outer box. Focus, hover, selection, targeting validity and unavailable state must remain visually distinct. Scene pins keep their type glyph, brass ring for required scenes, and red only for crisis status.
- Menus and modal choices announce themselves to assistive technology, move focus into the open panel, keep keyboard focus inside, close with Escape, and return focus when dismissed. Toggle choices expose their selected state.
- The presidential tablet should read as a consumer device: rounded 3D hardware, a glass screen, a national flag or arms wallpaper and a familiar home screen of app icons. Open each app inside the device with a short system-style transition and a clear route back Home. The tablet remains open at the last-used app/page while resting on the wood; mirror the actual device screen inside the same physical bezel and omit a label beneath it. Its opened view returns to that same screen state. News preserves newspaper mastheads and outlet selection. Social is a vertically scrolling timeline attributed to real publication names from the current edition. Do not invent account holders, replies, reactions or engagement counts. Reports opens an existing official report and marks it read. Home gives News, Social, Codex and Reports a clear icon-based entry point. Plan an introductory tutorial for discovering the tablet later.

## Design review decisions (G-01 — 2026-11-27)
- **Desk composition:** keep the bare timber on the right quiet. The resting consumer tablet is the only loose device there; no added folders, phone, nameplate or decorative clutter. The turn ledger is fixed player HUD stationery, never a paper prop placed on the table.
- **Map markers:** use compact, location-anchored enamel/brass tokens with readable category glyphs and distinct states. Red remains reserved for crises and harmful outcomes. Do not rely on color alone; avoid covering city and region labels. When several matters share a location, fan or cluster them around that location. Geographic selection follows the exact region/country contour, never a generic rectangle.
- **End Turn:** keep it fixed and tactile as part of the desk frame. Its blocked state must remain legible and clearly unavailable rather than fading into a low-contrast ghost. Put outstanding matter counts and the reason only in the button tooltip/accessibility label; never add a separate visible badge.
- **Screen targets:** prioritize desktop and a compact PC/Steam Deck baseline of 1280×800. Smaller widths should remain usable, with the ledger, tablet and controls deliberately arranged so they do not obscure the map; phone layout is a fallback rather than the art-direction target.
- **Dialogue occlusion:** preserve clean transparent character masters. Use room-specific foreground furniture layers (chair backs, table edges) when a seated character should sit behind an object; do not bake arbitrary room furniture into reusable portraits. Keep equal participant lighting and do not alter story content or scene effects.
- **Lara portrait:** do not generate her layered wardrobe/pose set until the author approves a base face. Preserve the current candidate as a candidate.
