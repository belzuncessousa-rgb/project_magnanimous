# C-34 — Seated portrait placement

## Intent

Keep seated characters large and high in the foreground. The room is ambience behind them. The portraits should occupy more than roughly half the screen height, with heads in the upper third; the lower edge may crop naturally.

## Reference

Use `docs/screenshots/collab/c29-seated-1600.jpg` and `c29-seated-1280.jpg` as the accepted scale/placement baseline. Reject the lower-placed variant: the author says those figures are too low.

## Direction

- Preserve or restore the large, high seated portrait scale. The characters take visual priority over the room.
- The right-side figure must always be mirrored inward to face the centre.
- If the seated pose looks strange, the character may bring their own chair as part of the portrait art. Do not add room-matched chair/table overlays.
- Standing portraits remain unchanged; equal lighting and clear faces remain required.
- Review the current author-approved rendering at 1600×900 and 1280×800. If main already matches this, close C-34 without a visual change; otherwise adjust only what is needed.

## Review evidence

Confirm the Blue Room at both PC sizes and spot-check one solo seated scene. Verify large/high foreground scale, inward-facing right portrait and clear faces.
