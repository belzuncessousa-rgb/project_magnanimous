# G-11 — Off-map flag tokens and country-name cartography

## Direction

Use small, tactile 3D flag pins to represent countries that are not drawn in the current map window. The existing map already places the Arennian Federation south beyond the canal and Zharan east across the sea. Their pins sit in the open-water space near the direction of those countries and open the same foreign-country inspection surface as a map selection.

Keep countries drawn on the map selectable through their actual land geometry. Do not add floating tokens on top of every country, and do not replace country hit areas with outlines or abstract emblems. The flag pin is specifically for off-map nations.

A shared hardware treatment keeps the pins in one family: a small cast-brass pin or short stand, a fabric/enamel flag with gentle perspective and one soft ground shadow. The national flag supplies the identity; do not add initials or a second animal/object emblem. A selected pin can receive a restrained brass glint or lift. Its hit area stays invisible and accessible by keyboard.

## Flag canon boundary

The current foreign-country canon names the Arennian Federation and Zharan, but does not define either country's flag, colors or official emblems. Do not infer or borrow real-world flags. The pin construction can be prepared now; country-specific flag art should follow the world-art decision and become its own approved asset.

## Country labels

Replace the uniform, floating title-case feel with cartographic typography that feels printed on the map surface:

- Country names use a restrained serif small-cap treatment with open letter spacing, warm muted ivory ink and a fine dark map-colored edge/shadow for legibility.
- Let label size and position respond to the country's land area; center names in clear interior spaces, with a slight deliberate stagger between neighbors. A short, gentle rotation is appropriate for elongated territories; don't force every label onto the same horizontal line.
- Keep labels unboxed. Avoid pills, plaques, pointers or oversized outlines on on-map countries.
- Preserve the hierarchy already agreed: Arvane view shows Kunasai as one nation; the Kunasai view shows the internal regions. Country labels should not obscure city names, rivers, map pins or political boundaries.

## Review target

Compare the continental and Kunasai views at 1600×900 and 1280×800. Check that off-map flags are clearly clickable but do not look like the game's main scene pins, country labels feel native to the map, and selection feedback follows the existing exact country/region geometry. No new world facts or gameplay mechanics are part of this pass.
