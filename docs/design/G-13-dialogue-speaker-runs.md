- Place a narrow colored binder rule at the left of the complete run. The name color and binder rule identify the speaker.
- Keep natural paragraph spacing inside the grouped run; do not merge distinct authored text into one paragraph.
- A narration/thought beat, a different speaker, choice, pause boundary or existing effect boundary ends the run. Do not group across those boundaries or change reveal timing, scroll behavior, text, scene IDs or state effects.
- Keep the existing visual distinction for narration and thoughts. Do not brighten or dim any participant.
