# C-37 — Open the main menu as a desk folio

## Evidence and problem

The main-menu capture in `docs/screenshots/collab/g02-menu-1600.jpg` and the current menu styles still show a generic ivory modal card over a blurred map. The author called this treatment a “creamy rectangle” and asked for a designed, slightly three-dimensional interface. C-15 correctly moved the persistent Menu and Actions controls onto the leather rail; keep that resting placement.

## Art direction

When opened, make the menu feel like a small presidential desk folio placed on the strategy table:

- Replace the generic rounded card with a layered, tactile object: dark emerald leather cover/edge beneath warm ivory paper, restrained brass corner or binding details, and soft contact shadows that make the page feel set down on the desk.
- Keep the surrounding desk and map perceptible. Reduce or remove the heavy blurred overlay so the folio appears to sit in the existing scene rather than replace it with a generic website modal.
- Keep the established Session and Preferences groups, labels and behavior. Render options as elegant unboxed rows on the page, separated by fine rules and clear spacing. Do not give every option its own pale rectangle.
- Selection and keyboard focus should use the established gilt diamond, brass edge or a paper-surface shift; never a rectangular outline. Hover, focus, disabled and destructive states remain distinguishable.
- Use the existing emerald, ivory and restrained brass palette. The result should feel weighty and official but quietly made, not ornate or glossy.
- Preserve the current close behavior, keyboard handling, accessibility, option order, and every existing action. Do not add features or alter game rules.

## Review

Capture closed, open and focused states at 1600×900 and 1280×800, plus the compact layout at 390×844. Confirm the object reads as a physical folio on the presidential desk, the map and tablet remain contextual, all options remain readable and usable, and no generic button rectangles or rectangular focus outlines return.
