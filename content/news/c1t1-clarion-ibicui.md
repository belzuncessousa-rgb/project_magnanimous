# A PRESIDENT FOR IBICUÍ — AND THE BILL FOR TEKOKU

She spoke of Katanaí. She spoke of St. Veldra. She spoke of the sertão and the jungle. And the North? THE WATCHMAN counted: Tekoku, which pays for this Republic, was mentioned exactly as often as decency requires and not once more. Tekoku has heard these speeches before. Tekoku remembers who sends the money and who sends the speeches.
