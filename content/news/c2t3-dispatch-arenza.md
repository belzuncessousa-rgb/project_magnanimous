# Kunasai and Arenza Agree to Talk Trade

ST. VELDRA — President Tomás Arguedas of Arenza ended a two-day visit with an agreement to set up a joint commission on trade and customs. Officials in Vassel welcomed it. The Arennian embassy said it 'supports all efforts at regional cooperation'.
