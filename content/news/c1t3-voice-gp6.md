# The Pipe Will Climb the Hill

It is official: the Great Sanitation Works will resume, and the trunk line that has stopped three streets above Vila Tamarin for eight years is to be finished. On the slopes, nobody is celebrating yet. Residents say they will believe it when the work starts. The Voice will report on progress.
