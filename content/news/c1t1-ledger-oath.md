# Vento Sworn In; the Easy Part Is Over

Lara Vento took the oath of office yesterday before a crowd the capital police estimated at "very large" and her party at "historic". The address was competent and, in places, moving. It was also, in the tradition of first speeches, careful to promise the weather rather than the harvest. The new government inherits a coalition it does not control and a treasury it has not yet been shown. The Ledger wishes it well and will be counting.
