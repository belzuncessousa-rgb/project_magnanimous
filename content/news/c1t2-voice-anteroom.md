# Forty Thousand Signatures in a Supermarket Bag

The Families of the Disappeared waited in an anteroom of the Ministry of Justice from seven in the morning. Their petition — forty thousand names asking for the dictatorship's archives to be opened — was received with courtesy and returned with a promise. Rosa Delmar said the families have received many promises from previous governments and will keep coming back.
