# Forty Thousand Signatures in a Supermarket Bag

The Families of the Disappeared waited in an anteroom of the Ministry of Justice from seven in the morning. Their petition — forty thousand names asking for the dictatorship's archives to be opened — was received with courtesy and returned with a promise. Rosa Delmar said she has a drawer at home full of promises, "filed by government". She added that the drawer is not yet full.
