# Lessa: the President Is "Open"

Senator Augusta Lessa left the palace yesterday saying that President Vento is "open" to rebalancing the Senate by population — a word the Senator repeated four times to reporters in the space of a minute, so that nobody could misquote it. The Ledger has long argued that a voter in Katanaí should weigh as much as one in Ibira. It welcomes the President's arithmetic, and looks forward to watching her explain it to her own deputies from Mognagna.
