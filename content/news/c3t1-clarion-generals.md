# THE GENERALS ARE WORRIED

Nine months of speeches against "armies in love with themselves". Old boats on the border. THE WATCHMAN has spoken to officers who say the armed forces have never felt so far from a government since the democracy began. General Voss, asked for comment, said only that he serves the office. He did not say the occupant.
