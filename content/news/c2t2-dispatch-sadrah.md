# Zharani Envoy Visits Katanaí

KATANAÍ — Zharan's special envoy, Nima Sadrah, met President Vento at the port of Katanaí to discuss financing for her government's great project. Neither side gave details. Arennian diplomats were reported to have asked for a briefing the same afternoon.
