# A Dry Year, a Polluted River

The Review's end-of-year summary: the driest year in the interior of Mognagna for two decades, record low levels on the Ibira, mercury in fish sold in Tekoku, and respiratory illness still rising in the old tobacco districts of Ibicuí.
