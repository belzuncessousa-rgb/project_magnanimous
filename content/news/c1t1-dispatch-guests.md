# Inauguration Guest List Reads Like a Map of the Contest

NEW VELDRANIA — The Arennian Federation sent a deputy secretary and a large delegation; Zharan sent a vice-premier, a smaller delegation and a twelve-page memorandum on port cooperation, delivered before the ceremony began. Arenza sent its foreign minister, who left before the reception. Mondrath sent a telegram. Diplomats in the capital read the front row — Arennians on the left, Zharani on the right, Arenza nowhere to be seen — as a fair summary of the year ahead.
