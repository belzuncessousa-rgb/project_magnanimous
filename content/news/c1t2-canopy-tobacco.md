# Old Tobacco Districts: Admissions Up a Third Year Running

Respiratory admissions in the former tobacco districts of Katanaí and St. Veldra rose again in December, according to hospital data compiled by the Review. Researchers point to a combination of urban haze and a smoking rate that has not fallen in a decade. Deputy Ana Sollis's bill on tobacco advertising remains unscheduled. A spokesperson for Trembolo said the company "shares the public's concern for clean air" and is investing in filters.
