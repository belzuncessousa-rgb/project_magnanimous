# The President's Mother Pays for Her Own Sewer

Celia Vento, seventy-four, retired school cook, has paid out of her savings for the last three hundred metres of pipe on her street in Vila Tamarin — the street her daughter grew up on and now governs from a palace. "It was my funeral money," she told the Voice. "I'd rather have a sewer." Asked whether the President approved, she said her daughter had been told.
