# A Country That Digs: Vento Bets the Term on Ibira

The President has chosen the rare earths of the Tauá Hills as her government's first great project — the boldest of the three options and, in the Ledger's view, the only one with a chance of paying for itself within a generation. The difficulty is not the ore but everything above it: forest, Indigenous land, and a river nobody in New Veldrania governs. Investors have expressed interest.
