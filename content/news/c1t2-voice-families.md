# The President Receives the Families

For the first time since the Amnesty Act, a sitting President received the Families of the Disappeared in person. Rosa Delmar, who has led them for twenty years, came out of the Ministry of Justice carrying the same supermarket bag she went in with. Asked what was said, she answered: "Nothing was opened. Something was heard." The Voice supports the families' demand.
