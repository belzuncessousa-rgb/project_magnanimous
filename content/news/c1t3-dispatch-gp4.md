# Zharani Firms Ask About Railway Tenders

NEW VELDRANIA — Representatives of two Zharani state construction firms requested meetings at the Ministry of Planning within days of the railway announcement. An Arennian diplomat, asked whether the Federation's firms would bid, said they "do not chase tenders, they are invited to them". In Arenza, President Tomás Arguedas observed that a railway to the eastern border "would be interesting, if it continued".
